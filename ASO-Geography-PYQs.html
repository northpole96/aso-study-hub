Part 01 · 7 questions2018: 2 · 2021: 4 · 2025: 1

Earth, rocks and landforms

These questions test how the Earth's crust moves and what that movement builds: plates, mountains, volcanoes, earthquakes and deltas. Most of them are textbook basics from Class 11 physical geography (NCERT Fundamentals of Physical Geography).

2018Q42

Which type of rock is granite?

  1. (A) Sedimentary
  2. (B) Igneous
  3. (C) Metamorphic
  4. (D) Organic
Answer B · Igneous

Granite forms when hot magma cools slowly deep under the ground. Rocks made from cooled magma are igneous. Basalt is also igneous, but it cools fast on the surface.

2018Q43

What is not correct with regard to earthquake?

  1. (A) It is caused by endogenetic forces of earth.
  2. (B) The magnitude of energy released by it is measured by Richter Scale.
  3. (C) The damage caused by it is measured by Mercalli Intensity Scale.
  4. (D) Epicenter is the point deep under the ground where it actually begins.
Answer D

The point deep underground where the quake begins is the focus (hypocentre). The epicentre is the point on the surface right above it. Richter = energy (magnitude); Mercalli = damage (intensity).

2021Q61

Which option is not correctly matched with respect to the plates and the type of plate boundary?

  1. (A) North American plate and Eurasian plate are diverging plates
  2. (B) Nazca plate and South American plate are diverging plates
  3. (C) Nazca plate and Pacific plate show a diverging plate boundary
  4. (D) Indian-Australian plate and Eurasian plate are converging plates
Answer B

The Nazca plate slides under South America, so they converge; that push built the Andes. North America and Eurasia move apart at the Mid-Atlantic Ridge. Nazca and Pacific move apart at the East Pacific Rise. India pushing into Eurasia built the Himalayas.

2021Q64

Match the type of mountain with the example.

  • (a) Block mountain — (i) Himalayas
  • (b) Old folded mountain — (ii) Black Forest
  • (c) Relict mountain — (iii) Scottish Highlands
  • (d) Young folded mountain — (iv) Appalachian
  • Codes: (A) iii i ii iv · (B) iii i iv ii · (C) ii iv iii i · (D) ii iv i iii
Answer C · a-ii, b-iv, c-iii, d-i

Block mountain = Black Forest (Germany), lifted between faults. Old fold = Appalachians. Relict (worn-down) = Scottish Highlands. Young fold = Himalayas.

2021Q65Check

Which region has the largest number of active volcanoes?

  1. (A) Alaska
  2. (B) Aleutian Islands
  3. (C) Hawaii Islands
  4. (D) Iceland
Answer Keys differ: B (Aleutian Islands) or A (Alaska)

The Aleutian Islands are a long volcanic island arc on the Pacific 'Ring of Fire'. Most of Alaska's active volcanoes sit on this arc, so Alaska as a whole holds even more. Coaching keys are split between the two. Hawaii and Iceland have only a few active volcanoes each.

2021Q66

Which river has a 'bird-foot' delta?

  1. (A) Danube
  2. (B) Mississippi
  3. (C) Niger
  4. (D) Nile
Answer B · Mississippi

A bird-foot delta has long fingers of land that push into the sea, like a bird's toes. The Mississippi brings very fine mud and the Gulf of Mexico has weak waves, so the fingers survive. The Nile has an arc-shaped (arcuate) delta.

2025Q43

Mount Lewotobi Laki-Laki, an active volcano, is located in which country?

  1. (A) Philippines
  2. (B) Indonesia
  3. (C) Japan
  4. (D) Malaysia
Answer B · Indonesia

It is on Flores island in Indonesia and erupted strongly in November 2024 and again in 2025. Indonesia has more active volcanoes than any other country.

Part 02 · 8 questions2018: 2 · 2021: 3 · 2025: 3

Atmosphere, climate and winds

This family covers what air is made of, how seasons work, local winds, cyclones and climate codes. Köppen codes and local-wind names are the favourite 'match the following' material.

2018Q41

Which is the largest constituent of atmosphere?

  1. (A) Methane
  2. (B) Oxygen
  3. (C) Carbon dioxide
  4. (D) Nitrogen
Answer D · Nitrogen

Dry air is about 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 0.93% argon and about 0.04% carbon dioxide.

2025Q85

Match the gas with its description (composition of atmosphere).

  • a. Oxygen
  • b. Nitrogen
  • c. Ozone
  • d. Water vapour
  • 1. Most variable gas, 0.2 to 4 percent
  • 2. Only 21 percent of the volume
  • 3. 78 percent of the volume
  • 4. Less than 0.00005 percent, unevenly spread
Answer a-2, b-3, c-4, d-1

Oxygen is 21%. Nitrogen is 78%. Ozone is a tiny trace (under 0.00005%). Water vapour changes the most from place to place (0.2% to 4%). The option letters were unreadable in the scan, so pick the option that gives this match.

2018Q81

Which would be the longest day in Australia?

  1. (A) June 21
  2. (B) September 22
  3. (C) December 22
  4. (D) March 23
Answer C · December 22

Australia is in the Southern Hemisphere. Around 21–22 December the Sun is over the Tropic of Capricorn, so the south gets its longest day. On 21 June it is the north's longest day and Australia's shortest.

2021Q67

Which local wind is not correctly matched?

  1. (A) Khamsin → North Africa and Arabia
  2. (B) Bora → Adriatic coast
  3. (C) Mistral → France
  4. (D) Samoon → America
Answer D

Samoon is a hot, dry wind of Iran (Kurdistan region), not America. Khamsin is a hot dusty wind of Egypt. Bora is a cold wind on the Adriatic. Mistral is a cold wind down the Rhône valley in France.

2021Q68

Match the name of the tropical cyclone with the region.

  • (a) Baguio — (i) China
  • (b) Cyclone — (ii) Philippines
  • (c) Hurricane — (iii) Madagascar
  • (d) Typhoon — (iv) USA
  • Codes: (A) ii iii i iv · (B) ii iii iv i · (C) iii iv i ii · (D) iii iv ii i
Answer B · a-ii, b-iii, c-iv, d-i

Baguio = Philippines. Cyclone = Indian Ocean (here Madagascar). Hurricane = USA / Caribbean. Typhoon = China and the West Pacific. Also remember Willy-willy = Australia.

2021Q69

Match the Köppen symbol with the climate.

  • (a) BWk — (i) Cold mid-latitude steppe
  • (b) BWh — (ii) Hot mid-latitude steppe
  • (c) BSh — (iii) Hot low latitude desert
  • (d) BSk — (iv) Cold low latitude desert
  • Codes: (A) iv iii ii i · (B) iv ii iii i · (C) i iii ii iv · (D) i ii iii iv
Answer A · a-iv, b-iii, c-ii, d-i

B = dry. W = desert, S = steppe (semi-dry grassland). h = hot (low latitude), k = cold (mid latitude). So BWk = cold desert, BWh = hot desert, BSh = hot steppe, BSk = cold steppe.

2025Q39

Statements on climate: I. Indian Ocean Dipole is also known as 'Indian Niño'. II. It is similar to the El Niño phenomenon.

  1. (A) Only I
  2. (B) Both I and II
  3. (C) Only II
  4. (D) Neither I nor II
Answer B · Both

The Indian Ocean Dipole is a see-saw of sea temperature between the west and east Indian Ocean, just as El Niño is a warming in the Pacific. A positive IOD usually helps India's monsoon.

2025Q66

Match the evidence of global warming (glaciers and ice).

  • 1. 400 feet every year
  • 2. Andes of Peru, 1978 to 1995
  • 3. Nearly 25 percent over the last 60 years
  • 4. Russian Caucasus, past four decades
  • a. Edge of the West Antarctic ice sheet is shrinking
  • b. Glacial retreat increased seven fold
  • c. Glacial ice has shrunk in the Tien Shan, China
  • d. Almost half of all glacial ice melted away
Answer 1-a, 2-b, 3-c, 4-d

West Antarctic ice edge shrinks about 400 feet a year. Glacier retreat in Peru's Andes became seven times faster from 1978 to 1995. China's Tien Shan lost about 25% of its glacier ice in 60 years. Russia's Caucasus lost about half its glacier ice in four decades. Option letters were garbled in the scan.

Part 03 · 9 questions2018: 3 · 2021: 3 · 2025: 3

Oceans, seas, rivers, lakes and grasslands

World 'records and odd facts' questions: the deepest point, the widest shelf, the river that crosses the equator twice, the sea with no coast. Every one of these has a short list of rivals worth learning together.

2025Q70

Which is the deepest part of the ocean in the world?

  1. (A) Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench
  2. (B) Horizon Deep in the Tonga Trench
  3. (C) South Sandwich Trench, South Atlantic
  4. (D) Oceanic trench in the Indian Ocean
Answer A · Challenger Deep

About 10,900 m deep, in the Pacific near Guam. The deepest point of the Indian Ocean is the Java (Sunda) Trench; of the Atlantic, the Puerto Rico Trench.

2021Q70

The widest continental shelf is found in the:

  1. (A) Arctic Ocean
  2. (B) Atlantic Ocean
  3. (C) Indian Ocean
  4. (D) Pacific Ocean
Answer A · Arctic Ocean

The Siberian Shelf in the Arctic Ocean is the widest in the world, over 1,000 km. The Pacific has the narrowest shelves because its edges are lined with trenches.

2021Q62

Which ocean current is found in the southern hemisphere?

  1. (A) Oyashio Current
  2. (B) West Wind Drift
  3. (C) Gulf Stream
  4. (D) Canaries Current
Answer B · West Wind Drift

The West Wind Drift (Antarctic Circumpolar Current) circles Antarctica. Oyashio (near Japan), Gulf Stream (USA to Europe) and Canaries (off North-west Africa) are all northern.

2018Q80

The 'Ninety East Ridge' is a submarine volcanic ridge located in:

  1. (A) Pacific Ocean
  2. (B) Atlantic Ocean
  3. (C) Indian Ocean
  4. (D) Arctic Ocean
Answer C · Indian Ocean

It runs north–south along the 90°E line in the Indian Ocean, for about 5,000 km. It was made as the Indian plate moved north over a hotspot.

2018Q83

Which sea has no coastline?

  1. (A) White Sea
  2. (B) Tasman Sea
  3. (C) Sargasso Sea
  4. (D) Japan Sea
Answer C · Sargasso Sea

The Sargasso Sea is in the North Atlantic and is bounded only by ocean currents (Gulf Stream, North Atlantic, Canaries, North Equatorial). It is famous for floating sargassum seaweed and eel breeding.

2018Q84

Which is the largest tropical lake in the world?

  1. (A) Lake Victoria
  2. (B) Lake Malawi
  3. (C) Lake Kivu
  4. (D) Lake Turkana
Answer A · Lake Victoria

Lake Victoria (Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania) is Africa's largest lake and the source of the White Nile. The largest lake of all is the Caspian Sea; the largest freshwater lake by area is Lake Superior.

2025Q80

Which river crosses the equator twice?

  1. (A) Amazon
  2. (B) Nile
  3. (C) Congo
  4. (D) Niger
Answer C · Congo

The Congo flows north across the equator and then bends back south across it again before reaching the Atlantic. The Congo is also the world's deepest river.

2021Q63

Which grassland is not correctly matched?

  1. (A) Prairies → North America
  2. (B) Pampas → North America
  3. (C) Veldt → South Africa
  4. (D) Steppes → European Russia
Answer B

The Pampas are in South America (Argentina). Other temperate grasslands: Downs (Australia), Canterbury (New Zealand), Puszta (Hungary).

2025Q53

Savannas are:

  1. (A) Temperate grasslands
  2. (B) Coniferous forest
  3. (C) Temperate deciduous forest
  4. (D) Tropical grasslands
Answer D · Tropical grasslands

Savannas are tall-grass lands with scattered trees, between the equatorial forest and the desert. Local names: Llanos (Venezuela), Campos (Brazil), the African savanna of Kenya and Tanzania.

Part 04 · 11 questions2018: 3 · 2021: 6 · 2025: 2

India: rocks, relief, rivers and climate

Questions on how India's land was built and how its monsoon behaves. The oldest rocks, the dividing ranges and the river sources come up again and again.

2018Q47

Where are the oldest rocks found?

  1. (A) Himalayas
  2. (B) Aravallis
  3. (C) Siwaliks
  4. (D) Indo-Gangetic Plain
Answer B · Aravallis

The Aravallis are the oldest fold mountains in India, made of very old rocks. The Siwaliks are the youngest Himalayan range, and the Indo-Gangetic plain is the youngest land (river mud).

2021Q71

Which one of the following geosynclines is the oldest?

  1. (A) Delhi geosyncline
  2. (B) Dharwar geosyncline
  3. (C) Eastern Ghat geosyncline
  4. (D) Satpura geosyncline
Answer B · Dharwar

The Dharwar system (Karnataka) is among the oldest rock systems of India, and it holds much of India's iron ore, gold and manganese.

2018Q73

Which range forms a dividing line between the Ganges Plain and the Deccan Plateau?

  1. (A) Aravalli
  2. (B) Vindhya
  3. (C) Satpura
  4. (D) Ajanta
Answer B · Vindhya

The Vindhyas run east–west just south of the Ganga plain and are the traditional line between North and South India. The Narmada flows between the Vindhyas and the Satpuras.

2021Q72

Which river does not originate from the Maikal Range?

  1. (A) Tapi
  2. (B) Son
  3. (C) Narmada
  4. (D) Mahanadi
Answer A · Tapi

Narmada and Son both rise at Amarkantak in the Maikal hills. The Tapi rises at Multai in Betul (Satpura range). Careful: the Mahanadi rises near Sihawa in Dhamtari, Chhattisgarh, close to but not strictly in the Maikal. The exam's intended answer is Tapi.

2021Q74Check

(i) The Gujarat Plain was formed by the depositional work of parallel consequent rivers. (ii) The Gujarat Plain contains the Cretaceous lava formations.

  1. (A) Only (i) is correct
  2. (B) Only (ii) is correct
  3. (C) Both are correct
  4. (D) Neither is correct
Answer A · Only (i), most likely

The plain was built from silt laid down by the Sabarmati, Mahi, Narmada and Tapi. The old lava (Deccan Trap) is found in Kathiawar and the plateau, not in the alluvial plain itself.

2021Q79

Which factor is responsible for fluctuation in Monsoon in India?

  1. (A) If the 2nd equatorial trough does not merge with ITCZ
  2. (B) Southern branch of the South-West Tropical Jet re-establishes over Northern India
  3. (C) Late disappearance of the southern branch of the Subtropical Westerly Jet from Northern India
  4. (D) Development of a strong low-pressure cell over the Tibetan landmass
Answer C

The monsoon can only burst after the westerly jet stream moves north of the Himalayas. If it stays over North India for too long, the monsoon is delayed or weak. A strong low over Tibet actually helps the monsoon.

2021Q75Check

Who among the following scholars did not delineate 'the climatic regions of India'?

  1. (A) Blanford, H. F.
  2. (B) Coppack, J.
  3. (C) Spate, O. H. K.
  4. (D) Singh, R. L.
Answer B · Coppack, most likely

Blanford, Spate and R. L. Singh all drew climatic-region maps of India. Coppock was a British land-use geographer, not a scholar of India's climate. Other names for India's climatic regions: Köppen, Thornthwaite, Stamp, Trewartha, Kendrew.

2021Q73

Which category of forests occupies the largest percentage in India?

  1. (A) Conifers and pines
  2. (B) Equatorial evergreen
  3. (C) Tropical deciduous
  4. (D) Desert vegetation
Answer C · Tropical deciduous

Tropical deciduous (monsoon) forests, which drop leaves in the dry season, cover most of India's forest area. Teak and sal are the main trees.

2018Q68

82½° E longitude is geographically significant to India because:

  1. (A) It determines the Indian Standard Time
  2. (B) It has a bearing on the tropical climate of India
  3. (C) It divides India into eastern and western zones
  4. (D) It enables determining local time in eastern India
Answer A · Indian Standard Time

IST is the local time at 82°30′E, which passes near Mirzapur (Uttar Pradesh). IST is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of GMT. The line also touches Odisha (near Koraput / Nabarangpur).

2025Q12

The hills situated closest to Kanyakumari are:

  1. (A) Cardamom hills
  2. (B) Nilgiri hills
  3. (C) Anamalai hills
  4. (D) Shevaroy hills
Answer A · Cardamom hills

From north to south along the Western Ghats: Nilgiri → Anamalai → Palani → Cardamom. The Cardamom hills (with Agasthyamalai at the tip) are the last range before Kanyakumari. Shevaroy hills are in the Eastern Ghats near Salem.

2025Q46

Ground water: I. India is the largest ground water user in the world. II. India's rank is 120 among 122 countries in the water quality index.

  1. (A) Only I
  2. (B) Both I and II
  3. (C) Only II
  4. (D) Neither
Answer B · Both

India pumps more groundwater than the USA and China put together. The rank of 120 out of 122 is from NITI Aayog's Composite Water Management Index report (2018).

India map: states, coasts, borders and passes

This is the 'look at the map' family. If you can picture India's outline, its borders and the Tropic of Cancer, most of these become free marks.

2018Q44

Which state is not bisected by the Tropic of Cancer?

  1. (A) Gujarat
  2. (B) Odisha
  3. (C) West Bengal
  4. (D) Rajasthan
Answer B · Odisha

The Tropic of Cancer (23½°N) passes through 8 states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram. Odisha lies fully south of it.

2018Q46

Which state has the longest coastline?

  1. (A) Gujarat
  2. (B) Andhra Pradesh
  3. (C) Maharashtra
  4. (D) Odisha
Answer A · Gujarat

Gujarat has the longest coastline (about 1,600 km by the old count; about 2,340 km in the 2024 re-measurement). Andhra Pradesh is second among states on the old count; Tamil Nadu is second on the new one. Odisha's coast is about 480 km (575 km in the new count).

2018Q72

In India, how many states share the coastline?

  1. (A) 7
  2. (B) 8
  3. (C) 9
  4. (D) 10
Answer C · 9

Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, West Bengal. Plus 4 coastal UTs: Daman & Diu (with DNH), Puducherry, Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar. (Options B and C were lost in the scan; the numbers shown are a reconstruction.)

2025Q67

Match the Indian states with the international boundaries they touch.

  • 2. China and Nepal
  • 3. China

2021Q80

Which mountain pass does not belong to the state given?

  1. (A) Bara Lacha La → Himachal Pradesh
  2. (B) Mana Pass → Uttarakhand
  3. (C) Jelep La → Arunachal Pradesh

2018Q48

Which state has the highest percentage of its geographical area under forest, as per the 2018 government report?

  1. (A) Madhya Pradesh
  2. (B) Arunachal Pradesh
  3. (C) Nagaland
  4. (D) Mizoram

By percentage, Mizoram leads (over 85%). By total area, Madhya Pradesh has the most forest. Among UTs, Lakshadweep has the highest percentage.

2025Q92

Which option correctly gives the top four states with the maximum increase in forest and tree cover (ISFR 2023)?

  2. (B) Madhya Pradesh, Mizoram, Chhattisgarh and Assam

2025Q93

Match the biogeographical regions of India with the description.

  • a. Trans-Himalayan
  • b. Himalayan
  • c. Semi-Arid
  • d. Western Ghats

Trans-Himalaya (Ladakh): sparse plants, rich wild sheep and goats. Himalaya: among the highest mountains, rich in species. Semi-arid: next to the Thar desert. Western Ghats: dense forests with many species.

2018Q74

The ADB-supported Integrated Kashang and Sainj Hydroelectric Projects are located in which state?

Kashang is in Kinnaur district and Sainj is in Kullu district, both in Himachal Pradesh.

2018Q70Check

For what is the Manas Sanctuary in Assam known?

  1. (A) Bears
  2. (B) Rhinos
  3. (C) Elephants
  4. (D) Bisons
Answer C · Elephants (usual key)

Manas is a tiger reserve, elephant reserve and UNESCO World Heritage Site on the Bhutan border. It is also famous for the pygmy hog, golden langur and wild buffalo. For rhinos, think Kaziranga.

Part 06 · 10 questions2018: 2 · 2021: 3 · 2025: 5

Odisha geography

Every paper now has two to five questions on Odisha's own land: its deltas, hills, soils, minerals, rivers and turtles. These are the questions outsiders miss, so they are worth the most time.

2018Q45

Why is Chilika Lake called a lagoon?

  1. (A) It is formed by damming of sections of river channels
  2. (B) It is formed by rain water flowing into local depressions
  3. (C) It is produced by erosional and depositional action of waves in the coastal region
  4. (D) It is formed by remnants of ancient ocean and sea areas
Answer C

A lagoon is a shallow body of salty water cut off from the sea by a sandbar built by waves. Chilika is Asia's largest brackish-water lagoon and India's first Ramsar site (1981, with Keoladeo).

2018Q62

What is not correct with respect to Olive Ridley sea turtles?

  1. (A) Gahirmatha is the world's largest known nesting ground of these turtles
  2. (B) Two other mass nesting beaches in Odisha are at the mouths of the Rushikulya and Daya
  3. (C) The eggs hatch at night and the hatchlings are highly sensitive to light
  4. (D) They are listed as Vulnerable in the IUCN Red List
Answer B

The three mass-nesting (arribada) sites in Odisha are Gahirmatha (Kendrapara), Rushikulya (Ganjam) and the mouth of the Devi river (Puri), not the Daya.

2025Q27

Which river in Odisha saw nesting of over 6.98 lakh Olive Ridley turtles during 'Operation Olivia' in February 2025?

  1. (A) Mahanadi
  2. (B) Brahmani
  3. (C) Rushikulya
  4. (D) Subarnarekha
Answer C · Rushikulya

Rushikulya rookery in Ganjam had a record mass nesting in Feb 2025. Operation Olivia is the Indian Coast Guard's yearly turtle-protection drive.

2025Q16

In Odisha, the 'Hexadeltaic region' is named after:

  1. (A) Six deltaic districts
  2. (B) Six coastal districts
  3. (C) Six rivers of the State
  4. (D) Hexagonal shape of the region
Answer C · Six rivers

The coastal plain is built by the deltas of six rivers: Subarnarekha, Budhabalanga, Baitarani, Brahmani, Mahanadi and Rushikulya.

2021Q76Check

Arrange the rivers by their contribution to the formation of Odisha's coastal plains.

  1. (A) Mahanadi > Brahmani > Baitarani > Rushikulya > Subarnarekha
  2. (B) Mahanadi > Baitarani > Brahmani > Subarnarekha > Rushikulya
  3. (C) Mahanadi > Rushikulya > Brahmani > Baitarani > Subarnarekha
  4. (D) Mahanadi > Brahmani > Baitarani > Subarnarekha > Rushikulya
Answer D

The bigger the river and its silt load, the bigger its share of the plain: Mahanadi first, then Brahmani, Baitarani, Subarnarekha, and the small Rushikulya last. (The question says 'ascending' but all options are written largest first.)

2021Q77

Match the rolling-upland units with the river basin.

  • (a) Rajgangpur–Panposh Uplands — (i) Ib Basin
  • (b) Jharsuguda Uplands — (ii) Northern Tel Basin
  • (c) Balangir–Titlagarh–Patnagarh Uplands — (iii) Sabari Basin
  • (d) Malkangiri Uplands — (iv) Koel and Sankh rivers
  • Codes: (A) iv i ii iii · (B) i ii iii iv · (C) iii ii i iv · (D) ii iii i iv
Answer A · a-iv, b-i, c-ii, d-iii

Rajgangpur–Panposh (Sundargarh) lies on the Koel and Sankh, which join at Rourkela to form the Brahmani. Jharsuguda is in the Ib basin. Balangir–Titlagarh–Patnagarh is in the northern Tel basin. Malkangiri is in the Sabari basin.

2025Q94

The Hirakud Project and Mahanadi Canal supply irrigation water to: 1. Puri 2. Cuttack 3. Sambalpur 4. Keonjhar

  1. (A) 1, 2, 3, 4
  2. (B) Only 1, 2 and 3
  3. (C) Only 1, 2
  4. (D) Only 3
Answer B · 1, 2 and 3

Hirakud's canals water Sambalpur, Bargarh, Subarnapur and Balangir. Water released downstream feeds the Mahanadi delta canals in Cuttack and Puri. Keonjhar is in the Baitarani basin, not served by Hirakud.
