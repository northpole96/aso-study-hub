This is the second mock on the 2021 layout. Seventy-five questions are new. The other twenty-five come back from Mock 02, spread across every block, so you can check whether last time's rules have stuck. Their options are shuffled again, so you have to remember the rule, not the letter. Like Mock 02, it follows the 2021 ASO English paper block by block. It runs in the same order: a mixed opener, sentence improvement, fill in the blanks, "choose the correct statement", synonyms and antonyms, word pairs, ten prepositions, ten articles, tenses, rearrangement, eleven agreement questions and a passage.

Two 2021 blocks are not in the official syllabus: parts of speech and analogies. They are replaced with adverbs, which jumped to 15 questions in 2025, and paragraph ordering, which grew from 1 to 20 between 2018 and 2025. So every question sits inside the syllabus printed in Advt. No. 15 of 2026-27.
