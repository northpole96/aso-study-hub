This is the second mock on arithmetic and algebra only: fifty new questions in forty-five minutes, at the same moderate-to-hard level as Mock 01. Each question takes a type the examiner has asked and adds a twist. Two are real past questions word for word (2021 Q55 and 2021 Q72). Every answer shows the working, the common trap, the past question it grew from and the next twist to expect. Press Copy answers at the end and paste the report into the chat for a redo set.
