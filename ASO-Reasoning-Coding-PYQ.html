These are all seventeen coding and decoding questions the examiner has set in Paper II: five from 2018, seven from 2021 and five from 2025. They are word for word and in paper order, with the options in the order they were printed. Coding is one of the steadiest sections, at five to seven questions every year.

There is no official answer key, so every answer was worked out letter by letter and the working is shown. The three kinds to watch are letter shifts, letters turned into numbers and added, and coded sentences. A few option letters were unreadable in the 2018 scan and are rebuilt; the explanation says where.
