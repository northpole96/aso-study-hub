This mock has one goal: to ask the questions most likely to come in January 2027. It copies the 2025 paper, the newest and the first to stay fully inside the printed syllabus: a long fill-in-the-blanks block, then error spotting, jumbled sentences, jumbled paragraphs and two passages, one of them about Odisha. Every question is the next step of a question the board has already asked. 2025 asked "No sooner … than", so this mock asks "Hardly … when" and "Not until … did". 2025 asked "A number of factors have", so this mock asks "The number of accidents has". Each answer names the 2025 or 2021 question it grew from and the rest of its rule family.

Synonyms and antonyms are named in the syllabus but were missing in 2025, after 11 questions in 2021, so six of them are carved out of the fill-in block in case they return. Narration, voice, analogies and question tags are left out: they are not in the syllabus and did not come in 2025. None of the 100 questions repeats Mocks 01 to 04.
