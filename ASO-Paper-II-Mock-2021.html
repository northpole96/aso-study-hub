This is the real 2021 Paper II, question for question. Questions 1 to 50 are Reasoning and Mental Ability. Questions 51 to 100 are Maths. Take it once under exam rules to see where you stand against the paper the board actually set. Question 10 and question 43 are the same question; the booklet printed it twice, and it is kept twice here.

There is no official key. The answers are worked out by hand. Nine questions (8, 9, 26, 30, 39, 42, 47, 49 and 77) could not be solved with confidence, because they depend on a crowded figure or have a faulty option list. Their marked answer is a guess and they carry a Check tag, so do not trust them. Figures are cropped from the scan.
