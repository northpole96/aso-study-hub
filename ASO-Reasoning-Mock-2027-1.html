This is the first half of Paper II on its own: fifty Reasoning questions in forty-five minutes, the time you get in the hall if you split the ninety minutes evenly with Maths. Every topic has its share from the 2018, 2021 and 2025 papers (2021 and 2025 at full weight, 2018 at half), and inside each topic the question families were drawn by how often the examiner uses them.

Fifteen questions have figures, as in the real booklet: series, mirror and water images, a net, a die, paper folding, counting, grouping and an embedded figure. Three are real past questions word for word (2025 Q43, 2021 Q20 and 2021 Q41), because this paper repeats itself more than any other. Every answer shows the working and the shortcut.
