This mock copies the real paper as closely as the past papers allow. The topic weights follow the 2025 paper, where the exam moved from a news quiz to a syllabus test. About thirty questions are in the longer 2025 styles: "consider the statements", "match the lists", "which is NOT correct" and "arrange in order".

Topics that the examiner keeps repeating, such as the Ilbert Bill, the Rolling Plan, the Earth Day theme, Dharma Guardian, the Kyoto Protocol and Pralay, come back here with a 2026 twist. Questions are mixed, as in the real paper. Your score is broken down by topic at the end.
