This is a short paper on one topic only: syllabus topic (h), Defence and Indian Armed Forces. In the real papers Defence gave 8 questions in 2018, 10 in 2021 and 12 in 2025, so it is one of the steadiest scoring parts. Here are 50 questions, each one the next angle of a family that the examiner has already asked: exercises and who hosts them, operations and what they were for, service chiefs, missiles, ships, border forces and gallantry awards.

About a fifth of the paper is 2026 news (the new chiefs, exercises of 2026, new ships). None of the 50 questions repeats a Defence question from GS Mock 01, 02 or 03. Every answer says which past question it grew from and what else to learn in that family.
