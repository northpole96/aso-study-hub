This is a practice set in mock style: fifty questions in forty-five minutes, in booklet order. The first twenty-two are Reasoning (number series, then ranking and order). The last twenty-eight are Maths: divisibility, time and work (pipes included) and trains. Each question is a likely next angle of a past question of 2018, 2021 or 2025.

The series use only rules that Number Series Set 01 and Set 02 did not use, plus a few new ones. Every answer shows its working and its type, so a wrong answer tells you which rule to revise. Press Copy answers at the end and paste the report into the chat for a redo set.
