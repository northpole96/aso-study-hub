This is a short paper on two topics only: the Indian Constitution, syllabus topic (d), and Defence and Indian Armed Forces, topic (h). In the real papers these two gave 21 questions in 2018 (13 + 8), 21 in 2021 (11 + 10) and 30 in 2025 (18 + 12). They are the two steadiest scoring parts of the paper, so they deserve a test of their own. Here are 40 questions, 20 of each, and every one is the next angle of a family the examiner has already asked: schedules, amendments, offices and committees on the Polity side; exercises, operations, ranks, missiles and ships on the Defence side.

About a fifth of the paper is news from 2025 and 2026 (the new Chief Justice, the Vice-President, Vande Mataram, the 2026 exercises, the Rafale-M deal). None of the 40 questions repeats a Polity or Defence question from GS Mock 01 to 04, the Defence Mock, GA Mock 1 or the GA Full Test. Every answer says which past question it grew from and what else to learn in that family.
