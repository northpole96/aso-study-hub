Speed questions took 6 of the 50 maths questions in 2025: Robert on his cycle (Q54), two trains passing a running man (Q59, Q82), a car going 10 km/h faster (Q64) and two races (Q60, Q65). This set covers every type in the family, with the twists the examiner likes: units, average speed, late and early, chases, trains against poles, platforms and other trains, head starts, circular tracks, and boats with and against the stream.

Three ideas solve almost all of them. Distance = speed × time. Multiply km/h by 5/18 to get metres per second. Speeds ADD when two things move towards each other and SUBTRACT when one chases the other. The four parts are mixed, so you have to spot the type yourself. Each answer names the type, shows the working, gives a shortcut and explains the trap options. Read the matching chapter of the Arithmetic Guide first if a type is new to you.
