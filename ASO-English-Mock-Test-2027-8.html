This full English paper (45 minutes) follows the 2021 booklet: a mixed grammar opener, then blocks of drills (six parts of speech, vocabulary, nine prepositions, eight articles, five tenses, ten agreement questions) and one reading passage. On your request it also has four voice-change and three direct-and-indirect-speech questions, and six rearrangement questions (four sentence parts, two paragraphs) written in small letters with no full stop, so the first part gives no clue.

The level is semi-moderate to hard: many options are close, and some questions need two steps. Three questions are exact past questions: 2025 Q58 (touching), 2021 Q55 (took it to heart) and 2021 Q64 (an ideal climate). Voice and speech are not named in the official syllabus list, so treat those seven as extra practice. The passage is on Sambalpuri ikat.
