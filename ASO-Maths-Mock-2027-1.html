This is the Maths half of Paper II on its own: 50 questions, 45 minutes, the same time you get in the real exam if you split the 1½ hours evenly. It was built by reading all three past maths sections from the scanned booklets. 2018 and 2021 used one Class 10 template: series of fractions, HCF, percent, interest, quadratics, A.P., matrices, then a long run of probability and statistics. 2025 changed course: almost all 50 questions were R. S. Aggarwal style arithmetic (work, pipes, trains, races, clocks, ages, partnership), and it copied one 2021 question word for word.

So this mock leans on 2025 (30 arithmetic questions) but keeps the syllabus parts that 2025 skipped: 9 algebra, 3 mensuration, 8 statistics and probability. Every question is the next angle of a real past question, and each answer names the question it grew from, gives a shortcut where there is one, and explains the trap options. Questions come in topic blocks, as in the real booklet. Keep rough paper and a pen ready; no calculator.
