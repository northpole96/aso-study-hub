What was asked

Pick a paper. For every topic, and for every family inside the topic, this page counts the questions that came in each year. A family is one kind of question that keeps coming back, such as "days and themes" in General Awareness or "time and work" in Maths. A dark box means many questions that year; a dot means none.

Click anything to read the real questions on the right. A topic or family name shows every year. A number shows only that year. A year at the top of a column shows that year for the whole topic list. Dotted underlines and outlined boxes are clickable.
