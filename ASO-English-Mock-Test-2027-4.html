This is a redo set built from your Mock 03 report. Every question you got wrong or skipped there comes back here: 21 in all, with the options shuffled again, so you have to know the rule, not the letter. Twenty-five of the new questions test the same rule as one of those misses with a fresh sentence. For example, after "plays the violin" you get "plays the flute", and after "some of the milk has" you get "some of the students were". The rest is a full paper in the 2021 order: a mixed opener, sentence improvement, adverbs, fill in the blanks, "choose the correct statement", synonyms and antonyms, word pairs, ten prepositions, ten articles, tenses, rearrangement, agreement and a passage.

Two 2021 blocks are not in the official syllabus: parts of speech and analogies. They are replaced with adverbs, which jumped to 15 questions in 2025, and paragraph ordering, which grew from 1 to 20 between 2018 and 2025. So every question sits inside the syllabus printed in Advt. No. 15 of 2026-27.
