This set goes with the Number Series Guide. It has thirty questions, one minute each, and it covers every pattern in the guide: gaps and gaps of gaps, alternating signs, two series mixed, multiplying, squares and cubes, the two terms before, letter series, and the 2018-style position and counting questions. The patterns are mixed, so the first job in every question is to run the checking order and spot the type.

Half the questions ask for the wrong term, because that is what the 2021 and 2025 papers ask most. Three are real past questions word for word (2025 Q49, 2025 Q1 and 2018 Q15). 2025 Q49 has no official key, so its answer carries CHECK. Press Copy answers at the end and paste the report into the chat for a redo set.
