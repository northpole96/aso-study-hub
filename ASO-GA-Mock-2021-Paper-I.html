This is the real 2021 Paper I (General Awareness), question for question. Questions 1 to 20 are current affairs from 2022, 21 to 30 history, 31 to 51 polity and administration, 52 to 60 international bodies, 61 to 80 geography, 81 to 90 defence, 91 science and 92 to 100 environment. Take it once under exam rules to see where you stand against the paper the board actually set.

There is no official key. The answers are worked out from standard books. About one question in four is tagged Check: the key may be wrong or the question itself is loose (a misprint, or two options that could both work). Do not trust a Check answer without looking it up. The 2022 current-affairs questions are old news now, so use them to learn the style, not to learn the facts.
