This full English paper (45 minutes) follows the 2021 booklet. It opens with a mixed grammar block of twenty, then six parts of speech, eight pick-the-correct-sentence and spot-the-error questions, six mixed questions, sixteen vocabulary questions, ten prepositions, ten articles, five tenses, four rearrangements, ten agreement questions and one reading passage.

The level is hard. Many options are close, and several words change their part of speech with their place (look, hard, enough, but, before). Each question still tests one rule. Three are exact past questions: 2021 Q13 (had never been), 2021 Q46 (SUBVERSION) and 2025 Q42 (focuses on). The passage is about the sea taking the coastal villages of Satabhaya in Kendrapara.
