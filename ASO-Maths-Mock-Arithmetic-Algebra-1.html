This mock covers only the first two topics of the Maths syllabus, arithmetic and algebra: fifty questions in forty-five minutes. The plan was drawn by probability from the 2018, 2021 and 2025 papers, so arithmetic gets 36 questions (the 2025 paper was almost all arithmetic) and algebra gets 14 (the Class 10 template of 2018 and 2021).

The level is moderate to hard. Most questions take a type the examiner has asked and add a twist: the unknown is reversed, a second condition is added, or two steps are joined. Two questions are real past questions word for word (2025 Q65 and 2025 Q76). Every answer shows the working, the past question it grew from and the next twist to expect. Press Copy answers at the end and paste the report into the chat for a redo set.
