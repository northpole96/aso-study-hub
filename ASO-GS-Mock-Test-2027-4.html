This is the third "most likely" paper, made the same way as Mock 02 and Mock 03. Every question is a topic family the examiner keeps coming back to, asked from the side that has not been asked yet. In 2025 the Uniform Civil Code was a wrong option in the Directive Principles question; here you are asked which Article it is. The 2025 paper asked who is NOT on the NHRC panel; here you are asked who is NOT on the Lokpal panel. In 2025 the Earth Day theme of 2024 was asked; here you are asked 2025.

The news questions copy the 2025 kinds: exercises and operations, new leaders, hosts of summits, themes of days, and which body sits where. None of the 100 questions repeats one from GS Mock 01, 02, 03, the Defence mock or the two older GA tests. Every answer says which past question it grew from and what else to learn in that family.
