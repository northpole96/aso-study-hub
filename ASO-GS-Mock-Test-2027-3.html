This is the second "most likely" paper, made the same way as Mock 02. Every question is a topic family the examiner keeps coming back to, asked from the side that has not been asked yet. In 2025 a wrong option in the Directive Principles question was "Organisation of Village Panchayats" and the answer was the 1976 addition; here you are asked which Directive Principle the 44th Amendment added. In 2018 the Treaty of Bassein was asked; here you match the other treaties.

The news questions copy the 2025 kinds: exercises and operations, new chiefs and leaders, hosts of summits, themes of days and new lists. None of the 100 questions repeats one from GS Mock 01, GS Mock 02 or the two older GA tests. Every answer says which past question it grew from and what else to learn in that family.
