This is the second full Odia paper: 100 questions in the order of the real booklet (correct spelling, synonyms, antonyms, then a grammar block of subject-verb relation, tense, verb and adverb, then sentence rewriting and one passage). Every question is the next angle of a past question, and four are exact past questions (2021 Q93, 2018 Q93, 2021 Q67 and 2021 Q77).

All the words, pairs and rules are new, so none repeats Odia Mock 01. The passage is about the Mahanadi and the Hirakud dam. Questions marked Check rest on a grammar book's naming that may differ from another book, so treat them as the book's key.
