History Practice

150 questions on British rule and the freedom struggle: no timer, no marks, only practice and review.

How it works

This is a question bank, not a mock. Every time you press Start, the questions come in a new random order and the options are shuffled again, so you cannot learn the answer by its position. After each answer you see the right option and a short explanation: why it is right, the question it grew from, and the facts of the same family that are likely to come next.

The 150 are two sets. Set A is the original 50 most likely questions. Set B is 100 siblings: if a set A question asks when the Dandi March began, its siblings ask about the Non-Cooperation Movement, the Rowlatt Satyagraha and the Cripps Mission. Questions you miss or skip are remembered in this browser, so you can practise only those later.
