This full English paper follows the 2021 booklet: a mixed grammar opener, then blocks of drills (six parts of speech, vocabulary, ten prepositions, ten articles, five tenses, ten agreement questions) and one reading passage. Sentence rearrangement is kept small (4 questions, as you asked), and the level is moderate: one clear rule per question.

Three questions are exact past questions, because OPSC repeats itself: 2025 Q2 (clear), 2025 Q51 (contrary) and 2021 Q38 (She lives in an apartment). The passage is on Chilika Lake. Every explanation names the rule and the past question it grew from.
