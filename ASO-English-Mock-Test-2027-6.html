This is a full English paper on the 2025 layout: 69 fill-in-the-blank questions, 9 error questions, 9 jumbled sentences, 3 jumbled paragraphs and two reading passages of five questions each. The topics and rules were drawn by how often the 2018, 2021 and 2025 papers asked them (2021 and 2025 count fully, 2018 counts half), and every question was then written by hand.

Three questions are exact past questions, because OPSC repeats itself: 2025 Q13 (Had she known), 2025 Q16 (selfish) and 2021 Q47 (sinister). One passage is on Odisha's Raja festival. Every explanation names the rule and the past question it grew from.
