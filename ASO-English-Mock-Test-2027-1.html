The 2025 English paper looked nothing like the 2018 and 2021 ones. There were no synonyms, idioms, voice or narration. Instead it had 64 fill-in-the-blanks, 6 error-spotting questions, 20 rearrangement questions and two reading passages. This mock follows that order and those counts exactly.

Inside the 64 blanks, the mix copies 2025 too: about 21 test word meaning in context and the rest test grammar, mainly tenses, agreement, adverb forms, "No sooner / Seldom" inversion, prepositions, articles and -ing forms. Every answer comes with a one-line rule you can remember.
