Q51A boy read 3/8 of a book on one day and 4/5 of the remainder on another day. If there were 30 pages unread, how many pages did the book contain? Check (the OCR lost both fractions)

(A) 240 (B) 300 (C) 600 (D) 412

AnswerA — Left after day 1 = 5/8. Left after day 2 = 5/8 × 1/5 = 1/8. So 1/8 of the book = 30 pages, and the book has 240 pages.

Q52One year ago, Promila was four times as old as her daughter Sakshi. Six years hence, Promila's age will exceed her daughter's age by 9 years. The ratio of the present ages of Promila and her daughter is:

(A) 9:2 (B) 11:3 (C) 12:5 (D) 13:4

AnswerD — The age gap is always 9. So P = S + 9. One year ago: S + 8 = 4(S − 1), so S = 4 and P = 13. Ratio 13:4.

Q53A contract is to be completed in 46 days and 117 men were set to work, each working 8 hours a day. After 33 days, 4/7 of the work is completed. How many additional men may be employed so that the work may be completed in time, each man now working 9 hours a day? Check (the fraction 4/7 is from the OCR's "4 … of the work"; 81 is the only option that fits)

(A) 85 (B) 90 (C) 81 (D) 80

AnswerC — Work done: 117 × 33 × 8 = 30,888 man-hours for 4/7. Left: 3/7, which needs 23,166 man-hours in 13 days at 9 hours = 198 men. Add 198 − 117 = 81.

Q54Robert is travelling on his cycle and has calculated to reach point A at 2 P.M. if he travels at 10 kmph; he will reach there at 12 noon if he travels at 15 kmph. At what speed must he travel to reach A at 1 P.M.?

(A) 8 kmph (B) 11 kmph (C) 12 kmph (D) 14 kmph

AnswerC — d/10 − d/15 = 2 gives d = 60 km. At 10 kmph it takes 6 hours, so he starts at 8 a.m. To arrive at 1 P.M. he has 5 hours: 60 ÷ 5 = 12 kmph.

Q55A clock is set right at 5 a.m. The clock loses 16 minutes in 24 hours. What will be the true time when the clock indicates 10 p.m. on 4th day?

(A) 9 p.m. (B) 15 p.m. (C) 11 p.m. (D) 13 p.m.

AnswerC — The clock shows 89 hours from 5 a.m. day 1 to 10 p.m. day 4. In 24 h the clock shows 23 h 44 min, so true time = 89 × 24 ÷ 23.7333 = 90 hours = 11 p.m. on day 4. ((B) and (D) are impossible, "15 p.m.".)

Q56What least number must be subtracted from 1672 to obtain a number which is completely divisible by 17?

(A) 6 (B) 7 (C) 8 (D) 4

AnswerA — 1672 = 17 × 98 + 6.

Q57If 15% of 40 is greater than 25% of a number by 2, then the number is:

(A) 12 (B) 16 (C) 24 (D) 32

AnswerB — 15% of 40 = 6. So 25% of N = 4 and N = 16.

Q58The sum of two numbers is 2490. If 6.5% of one number is equal to 8.5% of the other, then the numbers are:

(A) 989, 1501 (B) 1011, 1479 (C) 1401, 1089 (D) 1411, 1079

AnswerD — 6.5 a = 8.5 b gives a:b = 17:13. Sum 30 parts = 2490, so a part = 83. Numbers: 1411 and 1079.

Q59A train 100 metres long takes 6 seconds to cross a man walking at 5 kmph in a direction opposite to that of the train. Find the speed of the train.

(A) 55 kmph (B) 60 kmph (C) 50 kmph (D) 65 kmph

AnswerA — Relative speed = 100 ÷ 6 m/s = 60 kmph. Train = 60 − 5 = 55 kmph.

Q60In a km race, A beats B by 28 metres in 7 seconds. Find A's time over the course.

(A) 6 min 3 sec. (B) 4 min 3 sec. (C) 8 min 2 sec. (D) 5 min 6 sec.

AnswerB — B runs 28 m in 7 s = 4 m/s. B takes 250 s for 1000 m. A is 7 s ahead: 243 s = 4 min 3 s.

Q61Four different electronic devices make a beep after every 30 minutes, 1 hour, 1½ hour and 1 hour 45 minutes respectively. All the devices beeped together at 12 noon. They will again beep together at:

(A) 12 mid night (B) 3 a.m. (C) 6 a.m. (D) 9 a.m.

AnswerD — LCM of 30, 60, 90, 105 minutes = 1260 min = 21 hours. 12 noon + 21 h = 9 a.m.

Q6250 is divided into two parts such that the sum of their reciprocals is 1/12. Then the two parts are: Check (OCR shows "18 / 12"; 1/12 is the only value that fits an option)

(A) 30, 35 (B) 30, 20 (C) 30, 25 (D) 30, 28

AnswerB — Only 30 + 20 = 50, and 1/30 + 1/20 = 5/60 = 1/12.

Q63Four milkmen rented a pasture. A grazed 24 cows for 3 months; B 10 cows for 5 months; C 35 cows for 4 months and D 21 cows for 3 months. If A's share of rent is Rs. 720, find the total rent of the field.

(A) 3245 (B) 3260 (C) 3360 (D) 3250

AnswerD — Cow-months: A 72, B 50, C 140, D 63 = 325. A's 72 parts = 720, so one part = 10 and total = 3250.

Q64A car covers a distance of 715 km at a constant speed. If the speed of the car would have been 10 km/hr more, then it would have taken 2 hours less to cover the same distance. What is the original speed of the car?

(A) 45 km/hr (B) 50 km/hr (C) 55 km/hr (D) 65 km/hr

AnswerC — 715/55 = 13 h. 715/65 = 11 h. Difference 2 h. ✓.

Q65In a 100 m race, A runs at 8 km per hour. If A gives B a start of 4 m and still beats him by 15 seconds, what is the speed of B?

(A) 5.76 km/hr (B) 6.25 km/hr (C) 4.25 km/hr (D) 7.28 km/hr

AnswerA — A takes 100 ÷ (20/9) = 45 s. B runs 96 m in 45 + 15 = 60 s = 1.6 m/s = 5.76 km/h.

Q66If each side of a square is increased by 25%, find the percentage change in its area.

(A) 55.28% (B) 56.25% (C) 57.25% (D) 58.36%

AnswerB — 1.25² = 1.5625, so 56.25% more.

Q67If x = y^a, y = z^b and z = x^c, then the value of abc is: Check (the OCR lost the powers and the options)

Answer1 — x = y^a = z^(ab) = x^(abc), so abc = 1.

Q68The average weight of 16 boys in a class is 50.25 kgs and that of the remaining 8 boys is 45.15 kgs. Then the average weight of all the boys in the class:

(A) 47.55 kgs (B) 48 kgs (C) 48.55 kgs (D) 49.25 kgs

AnswerC — (16 × 50.25 + 8 × 45.15) ÷ 24 = (804 + 361.2) ÷ 24 = 48.55.

Q69A watch which gains uniformly is 2 minutes low at noon on Monday and is 4 min. 48 sec fast at 2 p.m. on the following Monday. When was it correct?

(A) 2 p.m. on Tuesday (B) 2 p.m. on Wednesday (C) 3 p.m. on Thursday (D) 1 p.m. on Friday

AnswerB — Gain in 170 h = 6 min 48 s = 408 s. To gain 120 s: 170 × 120 ÷ 408 = 50 h after noon Monday = 2 p.m. Wednesday.

Q70A number was divided successively in order by 4, 5 and 6. The remainders were respectively 2, 3 and 4. The number is:

(A) 214 (B) 476 (C) 954 (D) 1908

AnswerA — 214 ÷ 4 = 53 r 2; 53 ÷ 5 = 10 r 3; 10 ÷ 6 = 1 r 4. ✓.

Q71What is the sum of two consecutive even numbers, the difference of whose squares is 84?

(A) 34 (B) 38 (C) 42 (D) 46

AnswerC — (a + 2)² − a² = 4a + 4 = 84 gives a = 20. Numbers 20 and 22, sum 42. (Same question as 2021 Q93.)

Q72A man bought toffees at 3 for a rupee. How many for a rupee must he sell to gain 50%?

(A) 4 (B) 2 (C) 5 (D) 3

AnswerB — Cost of one = 1/3. Selling price = 1/2 each, so 2 for a rupee.

Q73If x men, working x hours per day, can do x units of work in x days, then y men, working y hours per day would be able to complete how many units of work in y days? Check (options lost; the usual options are x³/y², y³/x², x²/y³, y²/x³)

Answery³/x² — Work = men × hours × days. x³ units-of-effort give x units, so 1 unit takes x². y³ gives y³/x².

Q74An electric pump can fill a tank in 3 hours. Because of a leak in the tank, it took 3½ hours to fill the tank. If the tank is full, how much time will the leak take to empty it?

(A) 21 hours (B) 23 hours (C) 19 hours (D) 24 hours

AnswerA — 1/3 − 2/7 = 1/21.

Q75In a caravan, in addition to 50 hens there are 45 goats and 8 camels with some keepers. If the total number of feet be 224 more than the number of heads, find the number of keepers.

(A) 20 (B) 16 (C) 15 (D) 18

AnswerC — Feet: 100 + 180 + 32 + 2k = 312 + 2k. Heads: 103 + k. 312 + 2k = 103 + k + 224 gives k = 15.

Q77If 15 men, working 9 hours a day, can reap a field in 16 days, in how many days will 18 men reap the field, working 8 hours a day?

(A) 18 (B) 15 (C) 20 (D) 16

AnswerB — 15 × 9 × 16 = 2160 man-hours. 2160 ÷ (18 × 8) = 15 days.

Q78A sum of Rs. 800 amounts to Rs. 920 in 3 years at simple interest. If the interest rate is increased by 3%, it would amount to how much?

(A) Rs. 995 (B) Rs. 992 (C) Rs. 980 (D) Rs. 1050

AnswerB — Rate = 120 ÷ (800 × 3) = 5%. At 8%: SI = 800 × 8 × 3 ÷ 100 = 192, amount 992.

Q79Two numbers are in the ratio of 15 : 11. If their H.C.F. is 13, then the numbers are:

(A) 198, 143 (B) 195, 143 (C) 195, 141 (D) 198, 141

AnswerB — 15 × 13 = 195 and 11 × 13 = 143.

Q81A starts business with Rs. 3500 and after 5 months, B joins with A as his partner. After a year, the profit is divided in the ratio 2 : 3. What is B's contribution in the capital?

(A) Rs. 7,500 (B) Rs. 8,000 (C) Rs. 8,500 (D) Rs. 9,000

AnswerD — A: 3500 × 12 = 42,000. B: x × 7. 42,000 : 7x = 2 : 3 gives 7x = 63,000, so x = 9000.

Q82A train 220 m long is running with a speed of 59 kmph. In what time will it pass a man who is running at 7 kmph in the direction opposite to that in which the train is going?

(A) 15 sec. (B) 12 sec. (C) 18 sec. (D) 14 sec.

AnswerB — Relative speed 66 kmph = 55/3 m/s. 220 ÷ (55/3) = 12 s.

Q83If 3 men or 6 boys can do a piece of work in 10 days, working 7 hours a day; how many days will it take to complete a piece of work twice as large with 6 men and 2 boys working together for 8 hours a day?

(A) 6 (B) 7½ (C) 8½ (D) 9

AnswerB — 1 man = 2 boys. 6 men + 2 boys = 7 men. One work = 3 × 10 × 7 = 210 man-hours; twice = 420. 420 ÷ (7 × 8) = 7½ days.

Q84Three different containers contain 496 litres, 403 litres and 713 litres of mixtures of milk and water respectively. What biggest measure can measure all the different quantities exactly?

(A) 1 litre (B) 7 litres (C) 31 litres (D) 41 litres

AnswerC — 496 = 31 × 16, 403 = 31 × 13, 713 = 31 × 23. HCF 31.

Q85The sum of the ages of 5 children born at the intervals of 3 years each is 50 years. Then the age of the youngest child is:

(A) 4 years (B) 8 years (C) 10 years (D) None of these

AnswerA — Ages x, x+3, … x+12 sum to 5x + 30 = 50. x = 4.

Q87A crate of mangoes contains one bruised mango for every 30 mangoes in the crate. If 3 out of every 4 bruised mangoes are considered unsalable, and there are 12 unsalable mangoes in the crate, then how many mangoes are there in the crate?

(A) 460 (B) 430 (C) 480 (D) 450

AnswerC — 12 unsalable = 3/4 of the bruised, so 16 bruised. 16 × 30 = 480.

Q88In how many ways can a cricket eleven be chosen out of a batch of 15 players?

(A) 1365 (B) 1400 (C) 1465 (D) 1395

AnswerA — 15C11 = 15C4 = 1365.

Q89A man bought a horse and a carriage for Rs. 3000. He sold the horse at a gain of 20% and the carriage at a loss of 10%, thereby gaining 2% on the whole. Find the cost of the horse.

(A) 1400 (B) 1200 (C) 1500 (D) 1300

AnswerB — Horse x: 1.2x + 0.9(3000 − x) = 3060, so 0.3x = 360 and x = 1200.

Q90A sum was put at simple interest at a certain rate for 3 years. Had it been put at 2% higher rate, it would have fetched Rs. 360 more. Find the sum.

(A) Rs. 7,500 (B) Rs. 6,500 (C) Rs. 8,200 (D) Rs. 6,000

AnswerD — 3 × 2% = 6% of the sum = 360, so the sum = 6000.

Q91Two pipes A and B can fill a tank in 36 hours and 45 hours respectively. If both the pipes are opened simultaneously, how much time will be taken to fill the tank?

(A) 30 hours (B) 25 hours (C) 35 hours (D) 20 hours

AnswerD — 1/36 + 1/45 = 9/180 = 1/20.

Q92The number of girls in a class is 5 times the number of boys. Which of the following cannot be the total number of children in the class?

(A) 24 (B) 30 (C) 35 (D) 42

AnswerC — Total = 6 × boys, so it must be a multiple of 6. 35 is not.

Q93If (5 + 2√3) ÷ (7 + 4√3) = a + b√3, then: Check (the OCR lost the fraction layout; this reading matches the options)

(A) a = −11, b = −6 (B) a = −11, b = 6 (C) a = 11, b = −6 (D) a = 6, b = 11

AnswerC — Multiply top and bottom by (7 − 4√3). Bottom = 49 − 48 = 1. Top = 35 − 20√3 + 14√3 − 24 = 11 − 6√3.

Q94Due to a reduction of 6¼% in the price of sugar, a man is able to buy 1 kg more for Rs. 120. The original rate of sugar is: Check (options (A)–(C) were lost; only 10 and 12 survive)

AnswerRs. 8 per kg — The new price = 15/16 of the old. 120 ÷ (15p/16) − 120 ÷ p = 1 gives p = 8. This is not in the two surviving options, so the 6¼% or the money may be misread. See the scan.

Q95A fort had provision of food for 150 men for 45 days. After 10 days, 25 men left the fort. The number of days for which the remaining food will last is:

(A) 29 1/5 (B) 37 1/4 (C) 42 (D) 54

AnswerC — Food for 150 men for 35 days left. For 125 men: 150 × 35 ÷ 125 = 42 days.

Q96Two students appeared at an examination. One of them secured 9 marks more than the other and his marks was 56% of the sum of their marks. The marks obtained by them are:

(A) 39, 30 (B) 41, 32 (C) 42, 33 (D) 43, 34

AnswerC — The two differ by 12% of the sum. 12% of S = 9, so S = 75. Marks 42 and 33.

Q97Two pipes A and B can fill a tank in 24 min. and 32 min. respectively. If both the pipes are opened simultaneously, after how much time B should be closed so that the tank is full in 18 minutes?

(A) 9 minutes (B) 12 minutes (C) 8 minutes (D) 15 minutes

AnswerC — A alone for 18 min fills 18/24 = 3/4. B must fill 1/4 at 1/32 per min: 8 min.

Q98Find a number such that when 15 is subtracted from 7 times the number, the result is 10 more than twice the number:

(A) 5 (B) 7 (C) 8 (D) 6

AnswerA — 7x − 15 = 2x + 10 gives x = 5.

Q99The smallest number, that must be added to 1780 to make it a perfect square is:

(A) 70 (B) 68 (C) 69 (D) 65

AnswerC — 42² = 1764 < 1780 < 43² = 1849. 1849 − 1780 = 69.

Q100If the number 653xy is divisible by 90, then (x + y) = ?

(A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 4 (D) 6

AnswerC — Divisible by 10 means y = 0. Divisible by 9: 6 + 5 + 3 + x = 14 + x, so x = 4. x + y = 4.
