What stands out: this paper is heavy on basic grammar drills. There were 10 article questions in a row (Q63–72), 10 agreement questions in a row (Q83–92) and 10 preposition questions in a row (Q53–62). Three questions were copied word for word from the 2018 paper: Q7 (Ajita's party, prepositions = 2018 Q78), Q8 ("moot" = 2018 Q85) and Q9 (preposition matching = 2018 Q87). So OPSC does reuse old English questions.

Q1Change the active voice into passive voice: I will clean the house every Saturday. The house ____ by me every Saturday.

(A) cleaned (B) will cleaned (C) will be cleaned (D) None of these

AnswerC

Q2People ____ walk on grass.

(A) couldn't (B) needn't (C) mustn't (D) may not

AnswerC — A prohibition, as on a sign.

Q3Choose the correct word order:

(A) Why she did leave so early? (B) Why did she leave so early? (C) Why did so early she leave? (D) Why so early did she leave?

AnswerB

Q4Select the same meaning of the underlined word: Take care not to get hurt with the steam from the kettle.

(A) Burnt (B) Bruised (C) Scalded (D) Cut

AnswerC — Hurt by hot liquid or steam = scalded.

Q5____ his principles, he has to be very careful.

(A) With regard of (B) With regard on (C) With regard to (D) None of these

AnswerC

Q6He was seen ____ to the school.

(A) went (B) going (C) gone (D) go

AnswerB — In the passive, "seen + -ing" (or "seen to go").

Q7They organized a party ____ his honour. That Ajita was not invited ____ it, was a shame. She admired him most and it seems unlikely that she would forgive the organizers ____ their omission.

(A) at, for, towards (B) about, at, against (C) in, to, for (D) after, on, with

AnswerC — Repeated from 2018 Q78.

Q8When I say that the benefits of demonetization are moot, what do I mean?

(A) That I do not understand what the benefits are. (B) That I do not understand what demonetization is. (C) That the benefits of demonetization are surely seen. (D) That the benefits of demonetization are debatable.

AnswerD — Repeated from 2018 Q85.

Q9Choose matching prepositions to complete the following phrases: (I) According ___ the source (II) Different ___ the first draft (III) Based ___ this information (IV) Bored ___ the events (V) Consisting ___ cards. Prepositions: (i) of (ii) to (iii) by (iv) from (v) on

(A) I-(ii), II-(iv), III-(v), IV-(iii), V-(i) (B) I-(i), II-(ii), III-(iii), IV-(v), V-(iv) (C) I-(v), II-(iii), III-(i), IV-(ii), V-(iv) (D) I-(ii), II-(v), III-(i), IV-(iii), V-(iv)

AnswerA — Repeated from 2018 Q87.

Q10He had earned his well-paid job after a long struggle, but when he laughed at the most inopportune time, it cost him the job. List the verbs in this sentence:

(A) earned, struggle, laughed (B) had earned, laughed, cost (C) struggle, laughed, cost (D) job, laughed, inopportune

AnswerB — "Struggle" is a noun here.

Q11Which of the statements about the following sentence is TRUE? The Raos had been living so frugally all year that they saved enough money for a Mercedes.

(A) The present perfect continuous tense used here indicates a state that continues into the present. (B) The past perfect continuous tense used here indicates an ongoing state prior to a past action. (C) The perfect tense used here is not clear enough to justify the state indicated in the present. (D) The past perfect tense used here does not indicate an ongoing state prior to a past action.

AnswerB

Q12Choose from the options how the sentence can be improved: They could not admire his bright performance because of they dislike him.

(A) because of their disliking of him (B) because of their dislike him (C) because they dislike him (D) No correction required

AnswerC — "Because of" takes a noun, and "because" takes a clause.

Q13Choose how the sentence can be improved: She was uneasy because she never be on a plane before.

(A) had never been (B) never been (C) is never been (D) No improvement

AnswerA

Q14Choose how the sentence can be improved: They only work when they have no money.

(A) When they have no money, they only work (B) they only work (C) work only when (D) No improvement

AnswerC — "Only" goes next to what it limits ("only when").

Q15Choose how the sentence can be improved: I.T. experts may have to stop hunting for better job opportunities as these are not so easily available now.

(A) No correction required (B) may be stopping the hunting (C) should have to stop hunt (D) might have to stop hunting

AnswerA

Q16Choose how the sentence can be improved: The NYC building at New York City is as high as every other building in the United States of America.

(A) high than every other building (B) as high as every other building (C) higher than any other building (D) No improvement

AnswerC

Q17Choose the correct part of speech for the words in bracket: She (as well as) her sister is engaged.

(A) Conjunction (B) Preposition (C) Adjective (D) Adverb

AnswerA

Q18Choose the correct part of speech: This politician is notorious (for) corruption.

(A) Conjunction (B) Preposition (C) Adjective (D) Adverb

AnswerB

Q19Choose the correct part of speech: This golden ring is too (costly) to purchase.

(A) Conjunction (B) Preposition (C) Adjective (D) Adverb

AnswerC — "Costly" looks like an adverb because of the -ly ending, but it is an adjective.

Q20Choose the correct part of speech: A (drowning) man catches at a straw.

(A) Complement (B) Infinitive (C) Gerund (D) Participle

AnswerD — An -ing word describing a noun is a participle.

Q21Choose the correct part of speech: A great talker is a great (liar).

(A) Article (B) Verb (C) Pronoun (D) Noun

AnswerD

Q22Choose the correct part of speech: Eat (to live), but do not live to eat.

(A) Complement (B) Infinitive (C) Gerund (D) Participle

AnswerB

Q23Anita ____ for the charitable cause.

Q24Everyone at the party ____ amazed by his performance.

Q25Alice is not at home at the moment. She ____ work.

(A) was on (B) was at (C) is on (D) is at

AnswerD

Q26French people love cooking, ____ the English don't seem very interested.

(A) when (B) whenever (C) where (D) whereas

AnswerD — "Whereas" shows a contrast.

Q27Choose the correct statement:

(A) My aunt who lives in Mumbai is a doctor. (B) My aunt, who lives in Mumbai, is a doctor. (C) My aunt, who lives in Mumbai is a doctor. (D) My aunt who lives in Mumbai, is a doctor.

AnswerB — Extra information about a known person goes between two commas.

Q28Wait a minute, I ____ this box for you.

(A) carry (B) will carry (C) carried (D) was carrying

AnswerB — A sudden offer uses "will".

Q29It's wet and rainy outside today. You ____ go out without an umbrella.

Q33Change into passive: Everyone understands English. English ____ by everyone.

Q37Choose the correct statement:

Q41Doesn't it (intrigue) you? The word in the bracket means:

Q42The branches of trees were (fondling) the sky. The word in bracket means:

Q43What is the synonym of "veracity"?

(A) Inaccuracy (B) Reality (C) Redundancy (D) Falsehood

Q44Give the antonym of MILITARY:

Q46Choose the word most opposite in meaning to SUBVERSION:

Directions (Q50–52): In each question, four words are given, of which two are most nearly the same or opposite in meaning. Find those two.

Q50(I) Withstand (II) Climate (III) Hot (IV) Surrender

(A) (II)–(III) (B) (I)–(IV) (C) (III)–(IV) (D) (I)–(II)

AnswerB — Opposites.

Q51(I) Gregarious (II) Quixotic (III) Sociable (IV) Discernible

(A) (I)–(III) (B) (III)–(IV) (C) (II)–(IV) (D) (I)–(II)

AnswerA — Same meaning. Quixotic = idealistic and unrealistic.

(A) (I)–(III) (B) (III)–(IV) (C) (II)–(IV) (D) (I)–(II)

AnswerC — Repudiate = disown.

Passage (Q93–97)

A man is known by the book he reads as well as by the company he keeps; for there is a companionship of books as well as of men and one should always live in the best company, whether it be of books or of man. A good book may be among the best of friends. It is the same today that it always was, and it will never change. It is the most patient and cheerful of companions. It does not turn its back upon in times of adversity or distress. It always receives us with the same kindness; amusing and interesting us in youth, comforting and consoling us in age.

Q93Which of the following would be the most appropriate title for the passage?

(A) Books show the reader's character (B) Books as man's abiding friends (C) Books are useful in the youth (D) The importance of books in old age

AnswerB

Q94Which of the following is opposite in meaning to the word "adversity" occurring in the passage?
