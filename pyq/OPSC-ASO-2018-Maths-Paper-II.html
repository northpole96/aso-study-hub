Q511/(1·4) + 1/(4·7) + 1/(7·10) + 1/(10·13) + 1/(13·16) = ? Check (the OCR shows only the terms; the options were lost)

Answer5/16 — Each term = ⅓ × (1/a − 1/b), so the sum = ⅓ × (1 − 1/16) = ⅓ × 15/16 = 5/16.

Q52What least number of five digits is exactly divisible by 41?

(A) 10045 (B) 10004 (C) 10041 (D) 10025

AnswerB — 41 × 244 = 10004. (10000 ÷ 41 leaves 25, so add 16.)

Q53HCF of 9/10, 12/25, 18/35 and 21/40 is: Check (the options are garbled)

Answer3/1400 — HCF of fractions = HCF of tops (3) ÷ LCM of bottoms (1400). "1400" appears among the options, so the right one is probably written 3/1400.

Q57Two numbers are respectively 20% and 50% more than a third number. These two numbers are in the ratio:

(A) 2:5 (B) 4:5 (C) 6:7 (D) 3:5

AnswerB — 120 : 150 = 4 : 5.

Q58A fruit seller had some apples. He sells 40% and still has 420 apples. Originally he had:

(A) 588 apples (B) 600 apples (C) 672 apples (D) 700 apples

AnswerD — 60% = 420, so the total is 700.

Q59100 oranges are bought at the rate of Rs. 350 and sold at the rate of Rs. 48 per dozen. The percentage of profit or loss is:

(A) 14 2/7 % gain (B) 15% gain (C) 14 2/7 % loss (D) 15% loss

AnswerA — Selling price of 100 = 48 ÷ 12 × 100 = Rs. 400. Profit 50 on 350 = 14 2/7 %.

Q60The difference between the simple and compound interest on Rs. 4,000 for 2 years at 10% per annum is:

(A) Rs. 20 (B) Rs. 30 (C) Rs. 40 (D) Rs. 60

AnswerC — CI − SI for 2 years = P × (r/100)² = 4000 × 0.01 = 40.

Q61A and B can do a work in 8 days, B and C can do the same work in 12 days. A, B and C together can finish the work in 6 days. So A and C together will do it in:

(A) 8 days (B) 4 days (C) 6 days (D) 12 days

AnswerA — A+B = 1/8 and B+C = 1/12. Add them: A + 2B + C = 5/24. Subtract A+B+C = 1/6 (4/24): B = 1/24. So A + C = 1/6 − 1/24 = 1/8. 8 days.

Q62A man crosses a 600 m long street in 5 min. His speed in km per hour is:

(A) 3.6 (B) 7.2 (C) 8.4 (D) 10

AnswerB — 600 ÷ 300 s = 2 m/s = 7.2 km/h.

Q63(64)^(1/3) × ∛32768 = ? Check (the OCR lost the powers; this reading gives an option)

Q64If α, β are the roots of a quadratic equation such that α + β = 24 and α − β = 8, then the equation is: Check (the options were lost; an unplaced option block "2(1 − √3), 2(1 + √3), 1 + √3, 1 − √3" sits beside this page and probably belongs to the lost Q65)

Q66When x³ + 3x² − kx + 4 is divided by x − 2, the remainder is k. The value of k is:

(A) 2 (B) 4 (C) 8 (D) 6

AnswerC — Put x = 2: 8 + 12 − 2k + 4 = k, so 24 = 3k and k = 8.

Q67If (2x + 5y) : (5x − 7y) = 5 : 3, then x : y is:

(A) 50:19 (B) 56:63 (C) 48:56 (D) 16:25

AnswerA — 3(2x + 5y) = 5(5x − 7y) gives 6x + 15y = 25x − 35y, so 19x = 50y. x : y = 50 : 19.

Q68The fourth proportional to 5, 4, 25 is:

(A) 5 (B) 10 (C) 15 (D) 20

AnswerD — 5 : 4 = 25 : x gives x = 20.

Q71For which value of p does the pair of equations 4x + py + 8 = 0, x + y + 1 = 0 not have a unique solution?

(A) 4 (B) 2 (C) 1 (D) 3

AnswerA — No unique solution means a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂, so 4/1 = p/1 and p = 4. (The constants give 8/1 = 8, not 4, so the lines are parallel and have no solution.)

Q72The discriminant of the quadratic equation 2x² − 4x + 3 = 0 is:

(A) −4 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) −8

AnswerD — b² − 4ac = 16 − 24 = −8.

Q73If a pair of linear equations is given by a₁x + b₁y + c₁ = 0 and a₂x + b₂y + c₂ = 0 where a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂, then:

(A) The pair of linear equations is consistent (B) …is inconsistent (C) …is independent (D) …is dependent

AnswerA — The lines cross at one point, so the pair is consistent (and independent). Check — both A and C are true; the exam key is probably A. (2021 asked the parallel case a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂ with key B.)

Q74In an A.P. the 5th and 8th terms are 11 and 17 respectively. The 15th term is:

(A) 31 (B) 41 (C) 45 (D) 47

AnswerA — 3d = 6 gives d = 2. First term = 11 − 8 = 3. 15th term = 3 + 14 × 2 = 31.

Q76The sum of three numbers in A.P. is 36 and their product is 1140. Then one of the numbers is: Check (the numbers fit exactly: 5, 12, 19)

(A) 19 (B) 10 (C) … (D) 3

AnswerA — The middle number is 12. 12(144 − d²) = 1140 gives d = 7. Numbers 5, 12, 19.

Q77Find the sum of the first 11 terms of an A.P. of which the 6th term is 45:

(A) 445 (B) 495 (C) 497 (D) 485

AnswerB — S₁₁ = 11 × (6th term) = 11 × 45 = 495.

Q78A computer company must have 20 programmers to handle system programming jobs and 30 programmers for application programming. Of those hired, 5 are expected to perform jobs of both types. How many programmers must be hired?

(A) 50 (B) 45 (C) 35 (D) 25

AnswerB — 20 + 30 − 5 = 45.

Q82The least number which should be added to 2497 so that the sum is exactly divisible by 5, 6, 4 and 3 is:

(A) 3 (B) 13 (C) 23 (D) 33

AnswerC — LCM = 60. 2497 ÷ 60 leaves 37, so add 23 (= 2520).

Q83If a set has n elements then the power set of that set has ______ elements.

(A) n (B) 2ⁿ (C) 2n (D) None of these

AnswerB — 2ⁿ.

Q84Find the chance of picking an even number from the natural numbers 1 to 100: Check (options lost; the OCR shows "1…, 2, None of these")

Q85An urn contains a thoroughly mixed set of 10 white, 15 red and 25 black marbles. Determine the probability of drawing a white or a red marble: Check (the options are garbled; "None of the above" is option D)

Answer1/2 — (10 + 15) ÷ 50. If (A) 1/5, (B) 1/2 and (C) 3/10, the key is B.

Q86A solid metal cylinder of radius 14 cm and height 21 cm is melted down and recast into spheres of radius 3.5 cm. The number of spheres that can be made is:

(A) 48 (B) 56 (C) 63 (D) 72

AnswerD — π × 14² × 21 ÷ (4/3 π × 3.5³) = 4116 ÷ 57.17 = 72. Check — the OCR garbled options (A) and (B).

Q87How many times will the wheel of diameter 105 cm rotate in covering a distance of 330 m?

(A) 100 revolutions (B) 110 revolutions (C) 90 revolutions (D) 105 revolutions

AnswerA — Circumference = π × 105 = 330 cm. 33,000 ÷ 330 = 100.

Q88The area of the segment of a circle, if the angle of the sector is 120° and the radius of the circle is 21 cm, is given by:

(A) 190.953 sq. cm. (B) 180 sq. cm (C) 271.047 sq. cm (D) 117.041 sq. cm

AnswerC — Sector = 1/3 × 22/7 × 441 = 462. Triangle = ½ × 21² × sin 120° = 190.953. Segment = 462 − 190.953 = 271.047.

Q89A single letter is selected at random from the word PROBABILITY. The probability that it is a vowel is: Check (options lost)

Answer4/11 — The word has 11 letters; vowels O, A, I, I = 4.

Q90A and B are two events such that P(A) = 0.3 and P(A ∪ B) = 0.8. If A and B are independent then P(B) is: Check (options lost; one is "None of these")

Answer5/7 — 0.8 = 0.3 + p − 0.3p, so 0.5 = 0.7p and p = 5/7.

Q91A bag contains 5 brown and 4 white socks. A man pulls out two socks. The probability that they are of the same colour is: Check (options lost)

Answer4/9 — (5C2 + 4C2) ÷ 9C2 = (10 + 6) ÷ 36 = 4/9.

Q92Two dice are thrown simultaneously. The probability of getting a total score of 5 is: Check (options lost)

Answer1/9 — (1,4) (2,3) (3,2) (4,1) = 4 out of 36.

Q93A bag contains 8 red and 6 blue balls. If 5 balls are drawn at random, what is the probability that 3 are red and 2 are blue?

(A) 60/143 (B) …/141 (C) …/18 (D) 4/9

AnswerA — 8C3 × 6C2 ÷ 14C5 = 56 × 15 ÷ 2002 = 60/143.

Q94The G.M. of the numbers 3, 3², 3³, …, 3ⁿ is: Check (the OCR lost the options; they are of the form 3^(…))

Answer3^((n+1)/2) — GM = 3^(average of the powers) = 3^((1 + 2 + … + n) ÷ n) = 3^((n+1)/2).

Q95In a class of 100 students there are 70 boys whose average marks in a single subject are 75. If the average mark of the complete class is 72, then the average mark of the girls is:

(A) 73 (B) 65 (C) 68 (D) 74

AnswerB — 100 × 72 = 7200. Boys 70 × 75 = 5250. Girls 1950 ÷ 30 = 65.

Q96The mean (OCR shows "sum") of 7 variates is 12. If six of them are 5, 13, 9, 17, 14 and 10, the 7th variate is: Check (the OCR says "sum is 12", which cannot work: the six already add to 68)
