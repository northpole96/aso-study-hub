This mock has one rule: every question is here because it is likely to be asked in the coming paper. Each of the 300 questions of 2018, 2021 and 2025 was sorted into a topic family. The examiner comes back to the same families but changes the angle, so this paper asks the next angle. In 2025 a false statement said Motilal Nehru led the Karachi Session; here you are asked who really did.

The news questions copy the 2025 pattern too: exercises and operations, themes of days, who was elected or appointed, and new lists such as World Heritage Sites. All 100 questions are new, and none is repeated from the older tests. Every answer tells you which past question it grew from and what else to learn in that family.
