This is the first full Odia paper in the set: 100 questions in the order of the real booklet (correct spelling, synonyms, antonyms, then a grammar block of subject-verb relation, tense, verb and adverb, then sentence rewriting and one passage). Every question is the next angle of a question asked in 2018, 2021 or 2025, and four are exact old questions.

Spelling, synonyms and antonyms come first because they are about a third of every real paper. The verb is the biggest grammar block (29 questions). Questions marked Check rest on a grammar book's naming that may differ from another book, so treat them with care.
