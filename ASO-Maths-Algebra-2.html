Algebra was 15 questions in 2018 and 14 in 2021, but only 5 in 2025. It follows one Class 10 template, so the same families come back with new numbers: identities, linear equations, pairs of equations, the roots α and β, nature of roots, quadratic word problems, A.P. and series of fractions. This is the second set: 50 new questions across ten families, mixed, with none repeated from Set 01. You must spot the family yourself.

Each answer names the family, shows the working, and explains the trap option. Keep rough paper ready; no calculator.
