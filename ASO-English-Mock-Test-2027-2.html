This mock follows the 2021 ASO English paper block by block. It runs in the same order: a mixed opener, sentence improvement, fill in the blanks, "choose the correct statement", synonyms and antonyms, word pairs, ten prepositions, ten articles, tenses, rearrangement, eleven agreement questions and a passage.

Two 2021 blocks are not in the official syllabus: parts of speech and analogies. They are replaced with adverbs, which jumped to 15 questions in 2025, and paragraph ordering, which grew from 1 to 20 between 2018 and 2025. So every question sits inside the syllabus printed in Advt. No. 15 of 2026-27.
