This set goes with the number-system chapters of the Arithmetic Guide. It has thirty questions, one minute each, and covers every type in those chapters: divisibility with unknown digits, least number to add or take away, counting multiples, successive division, last digits of powers, HCF and LCM in all their remainder forms, fractions, consecutive numbers, two-digit numbers, factors and decimals.

Each type comes with a twist the papers have not used yet, so you cannot answer from memory. Three questions are real past questions word for word (2025 Q71, which was also 2021 Q93; 2025 Q100; 2018 Q82). Press Copy answers at the end and paste the report into the chat for a redo set.
