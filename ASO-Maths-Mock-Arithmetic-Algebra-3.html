The 2018 and 2021 maths papers used one Class 10 template, slot by slot: a fraction series, a divisibility question, the H.C.F. of fractions, a decimal product, an identity with big numbers, recurring decimals, percentages, profit and loss, CI minus SI, work, speed and a root, and then the algebra block of roots, indices, ratios, matrices, linear pairs, the discriminant and the A.P. This mock follows that template exactly and keeps its difficulty: short questions, one idea each, no long word problems.

The arithmetic block runs through the slots twice, once in the style of 2018 and once in the style of 2021, with four extras of the counting and averages kind. The algebra block runs through the slots once and then adds a second question for the roots, linear pairs, A.P. and sets slots. Three questions are real past questions word for word (2021 Q51, Q68 and Q76). Press Copy answers at the end and paste the report into the chat for a redo set.
