#CH start | Start here | READ FIRST · 3 minutes
This guide covers the last two blocks of the maths booklet: **probability** and **statistics**. We go through probability first. In 2018 and 2021 together they were 17 and 16 of the 50 maths questions, which is one third of the maths paper. The 2025 paper had none, because that year the paper was all word problems. The 2027 paper is for the same post as 2018 and 2021, so we should expect the older style to come back. ⟨CHECK⟩ Compare with the real paper when it is out.

Both blocks are easy marks for one reason: the questions repeat. In both years the slots were the same, and only the numbers changed. Learn the types below and these 16 questions become a gift.

##How the guide goes
Chapters 1 to 4 are probability, the part to study first. Chapters 5 to 9 are statistics. Chapter 10 is a one-page sheet and a list of what is likely to come next. Every chapter starts with the words you need, then the rule with its reason, then worked examples (do each one on rough paper before you read the working), and ends with likely-next questions that you open by tapping.

>KEY:The two ideas behind everything here
**Statistics** is about a list of numbers: what is its middle, and how spread out is it? **Probability** is about a chance: out of everything that can happen, how much do we want? Almost every question reduces to a sum, a count or a fraction.
