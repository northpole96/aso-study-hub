#CH airforce | The Indian Air Force | PART A · STATIC
The Air Force guards the sky and supports the Army and Navy. **Air Force Day** is on **8 October**. The force was formed on 8 October **1932**, and it became the Indian Air Force (dropping "Royal") in 1950. The 94th Air Force Day was held on 8 October 2026 at **Hindon**, Ghaziabad. The motto is "Nabhah Sprisham Deeptam", "Touch the sky with glory".

##Air defence and others
- **S-400 Triumf** from Russia. Reported plan: five systems in all, with more cleared by the DAC. In February 2026 the DAC cleared 288 S-400 missiles (about ₹10,000 crore) to refill the stocks used in Operation Sindoor ⟨CHECK⟩. The air force uses the name **Sudarshan Chakra** for the S-400.
- **Akash**, **MRSAM** and the new **Kusha** make the layers.
- **Akashteer** is the Army's air defence control network.
- **Garud Commando Force:** the Air Force's special forces, raised in 2004.
- **Air Force Academy:** Dundigal, near Hyderabad.
- **Aero India:** the air show at Yelahanka, Bengaluru. The 15th was in February 2025. The 16th is scheduled for 8 to 12 February 2027 ⟨CHECK⟩.
- A **Squadron Leader** equals an Army **Major** ⟦2021 Q90⟧.

>WARN:The Captain trap
A **Navy Captain** is senior: equal to an Army **Colonel** and an Air Force **Group Captain**. An **Army Captain** is a junior officer, just above a Lieutenant.

#CH capf | Border forces and internal security | PART A · STATIC
India has about **15,106 km** of land border with seven neighbours and a coastline of about **7,516 km**. The **Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs)** guard the borders in peace and keep order inside the country. They work under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**, not the Ministry of Defence. The **Indian Coast Guard** is the exception: it is under Defence.

##The forces
|Force|Raised|Job and border|Motto|
|---|---|---|---|
|Assam Rifles|1835 (Cachar Levy)|Oldest. India–Myanmar border (1,643 km). Headed by an Army officer. Administered by Home Affairs, operations under the Army. HQ Shillong|Friends of the Hill People|
|Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF)|1939 (27 July, the Crown Representative's Police)|Largest. Keeps peace inside the country. Units: CoBRA (jungle warfare), Rapid Action Force. **Valour Day** is 9 April, for the 1965 Sardar Post fight in the Rann of Kutch|Service and Loyalty|
|Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP)|24 October 1962, after the war with China|India–China border (3,488 km) in high mountains. Disaster relief too. ⟦2025 Q29⟧|Shaurya, Dridhta, Karma Nishtha (Valour, Determination, Devotion to Duty)|
|Border Security Force (BSF)|1 December 1965, after the war with Pakistan|India–Pakistan (3,323 km) and India–Bangladesh (4,096 km) borders. First Director General: K. F. Rustamji|Duty unto Death|
|Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB)|1963 (as Special Service Bureau)|India–Nepal (1,751 km) and India–Bhutan (699 km) open borders|Service, Security, Brotherhood|
|Central Industrial Security Force (CISF)|10 March 1969|Airports, Delhi Metro, atomic plants, Parliament, ports. A port security vertical was planned in 2026|Protection and Security|
|National Security Guard (NSG)|1984|"Black Cats". Counter-terror and hijack work. Base at Manesar. First **Mahila Commando Conversion Course** launched at Manesar in August 2026 ⟨CHECK⟩|Sarvatra Sarvottam Suraksha|
|National Disaster Response Force (NDRF)|2006 (Disaster Management Act 2005)|Floods, quakes, building collapse|Disaster Response|

>KEY:One border, one force
Pakistan and Bangladesh: **BSF**. China: **ITBP**. Nepal and Bhutan: **SSB**. Myanmar: **Assam Rifles**. Inside the country: **CRPF**. Airports and metro: **CISF**. Sea: **Coast Guard**.

>WARN:The ITBP question of 2025
Two statements were true: it was formed after the 1962 war, and it helps in disasters. The false one said it opened a new base at "Kutul, Rajasthan". **Kutul is in Chhattisgarh**, against Naxalites. The examiner changed the State and the purpose. ⟦2025 Q29⟧

##Latest on the CAPFs
- **Zero Line:** the strip along the India–Bangladesh border, used in the term "Operation Zero Line". ⟦2025 Q20⟧
- **Vibrant Villages Programme II:** approved in April 2025 for about ₹6,839 crore to 2028–29, for 1,954 border villages on the Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh, Bhutan and Myanmar borders.
- **Smart Border project:** announced by the Home Minister for the Pakistan and Bangladesh borders (about 6,000 km) ⟨CHECK⟩.
- **PRAHAAR:** the National Counter-Terrorism Policy and Strategy released by the Home Ministry in February 2026.
- **NIDMS:** the National IED Data Management System, launched in January 2026.
- **CAPF Bill 2026:** a single law for CRPF, BSF, ITBP and CISF, which gives more senior posts to IPS officers ⟨CHECK⟩.
- **New Directors General (January 2026):** BSF: Praveen Kumar. ITBP: Shatrujeet Singh Kapoor ⟨CHECK⟩.

##The BRO
The **Border Roads Organisation** was raised in **1960** and now works under the Ministry of Defence. It builds roads, bridges and tunnels in the hills: **Atal Tunnel** (Rohtang, Himachal Pradesh, opened 3 October 2020, the longest highway tunnel above 10,000 feet), **Sela Tunnel** (Arunachal Pradesh, near Tawang ⟦2021 Q89⟧), and the **Shinku La** tunnel under construction. Its projects have names: Himank, Vijayak, Deepak, Dantak (Bhutan), Chetak (Rajasthan), Udayak, Arunank.

#CH weapons | Weapons, DRDO and the defence industry | PART A · STATIC
##Missiles
A **ballistic** missile is pushed up by its rocket and falls on the target like a thrown stone. A **cruise** missile flies low and level, like an unmanned plane. **Supersonic** is faster than sound (about Mach 1 to 5). **Hypersonic** is above **Mach 5**.

>WARN:The Pralay trap
In 2025 two false statements were set: "air-to-surface cruise missile" and "700 to 1,500 km, naval". Pralay is a **surface-to-surface quasi-ballistic** missile of **150 to 500 km**, for the Army and the Air Force. ⟦2025 Q36⟧

>KEY:Start and target
Name each missile by where it starts and what it hits. **Agni**: land to land. **Akash**: ground to air. **Astra**: air to air. **Nag**: at tanks. **Rudram**: air to radar. The examiner changes one word, so check both halves.

##Scramjet and hypersonic
A **scramjet** burns fuel in air that moves faster than sound inside the engine, with no moving parts. ⟦2025 Q51⟧ DRDL, Hyderabad, ran a long-duration ground test of the actively cooled scramjet full-scale combustor on 9 January 2026 for more than 12 minutes. In 2020 DRDO flew the HSTDV at Mach 6.

##Hit by name: the Missile Man and the Missile Woman
- **Dr A. P. J. Abdul Kalam:** the "Missile Man". He led the **Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP)** from 1983. It gave Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Trishul and Nag. He was Scientific Adviser to the Defence Minister and DRDO head (1992 to 1999).
- **Dr Tessy Thomas:** the "Missile Woman of India", who led the Agni projects. ⟦2021 Q84⟧

##DRDO

##Self-reliance (Atmanirbhar) in defence
- **Positive Indigenisation Lists:** items the forces may not import. The first was in August 2020.
- **iDEX** (Innovations for Defence Excellence), 2018: helps start-ups and small firms. Backed by the **Technology Development Fund**.
- **Defence Industrial Corridors:** Uttar Pradesh and Tamil Nadu.
- **FDI:** 74% by the automatic route since 2020 (it was 49%).
- **Money (2026–27):** defence budget about **₹7.85 lakh crore** (₹7,84,678 crore), about 15% more than the year before. Capital outlay about **₹2.19 lakh crore**. About ₹1.39 lakh crore (75% of the capital buying money) is kept for Indian industry.
- **Production** in 2025–26 reached a record about **₹1.78 lakh crore**. **Exports** reached a record **₹38,424 crore** (up about 62.7%). Private firms gave about 45%. The target is ₹50,000 crore. India sells to over 80 countries.
- **SIPRI (2025 figures):** India is the **fifth-largest spender** (about US$92.1 billion) and the **second-largest arms importer** (2021–25). Russia supplied about 40% of imports, France about 29% and Israel about 15%.
