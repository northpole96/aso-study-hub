#CH inv | 2. "Only when" at the start | WEAK SPOT · Q4
When **Only when, Only after, Only then, Not until, Never, Seldom, Hardly, No sooner** opens a sentence, the main clause takes **question order**: helping verb before the subject. If there is no helping verb, add **do / does / did**. The 2025 paper asked this four times ⟦2025 Q8⟧ ⟦2025 Q40⟧.

✗ Only when the lights came back on we saw the damage.
✓ Only when the lights came back on did we see the damage.
✓ Only after the doctor checked him was he allowed home.

The "when" part keeps normal order. Only the **main** part turns round.

#CH verb | 3. would rather, deny, lest | WEAK SPOT · Q12, Q19, Q29
These words fix the **shape** of the next verb. Learn each as a frame.

|Frame|Rule|Example|
|---|---|---|
|would rather + **you / he / they**|past form|I would rather you **did not mention** it.|
|would rather (my own action)|base verb|I would rather **stay** home.|
|deny, admit, avoid, mind, enjoy, finish, risk|+ -ing (never "to")|He denied **having been** there.|
|lest (= so that … not)|+ should + base verb; no extra "not"|Walk carefully lest you **should fall**.|

#CH imp | 4. No improvement; "the reason is that" | WEAK SPOT · Q10, Q14
Two opposite lessons from sentence improvement ⟦2021 Q12⟧ ⟦2021 Q13⟧.

##Lesson 1: sometimes nothing is wrong
Try the sentence **as it is** first. If it is correct and every option breaks it, choose **No improvement**. The frame "The more …, **the** less …" needs *the* in both halves and the plain word *less*.
✓ The more you practise, the less nervous you will feel.
✗ The more you practise, less nervous you will feel.

##Lesson 2: don't say "reason" twice
*Reason* and *because* mean the same thing. Write **the reason is that**.
✗ The reason he failed is because he did not read the questions.
✓ The reason he failed is that he did not read the questions.

Also: **because of** + noun (because of his illness); **because** + clause (because he was ill).

#CH who | 5. who, with "I thought" in the middle | WEAK SPOT · Q28
**Who** does the action (subject). **Whom** receives it (object). When *I thought, we believe, they said* sits in the middle, **cover it** and read again.

The man who/whom ~~I thought~~ was honest → "the man **who** was honest". So **who**.

✗ The man whom I thought was honest cheated me.
✓ The man who I thought was honest cheated me.
✓ The man whom I trusted cheated me. (I trusted **him**: object)

#CH pos | 7. before, but, seeing | WEAK SPOT · Q22, Q25, Q26
A word's **job in the sentence** decides its part of speech ⟦2021 Q17–22⟧.

|Word|Test|Part of speech|
|---|---|---|
|before / after / since|nothing follows|Adverb: I have met him **before**.|
|before / after / since|a noun follows|Preposition: **before** lunch.|
