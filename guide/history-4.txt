#CH groups | Muslim politics, caste, labour, peasants and women | PART A · STATIC
This chapter holds the families that grew in 2025: left parties, labour bodies, and who founded what. In 2025 four questions came from this area (N. M. Joshi, Labour Swaraj Party twice, M. N. Roy).

##Muslim politics and the idea of Pakistan
- **Sir Syed Ahmad Khan** told Muslims to take English education and stay away from the Congress.
- **Muslim League (1906)** and **separate electorates (1909)**.
- **Khilafat and Non-Cooperation** (1919–22) brought Hindu–Muslim unity for a short while. After Chauri Chaura the unity broke.
- **Muhammad Iqbal**, in his presidential speech to the League at **Allahabad (1930)**, suggested a Muslim state in the north-west. **Chaudhary Rahmat Ali** (Cambridge, **1933**, pamphlet "**Now or Never**") coined the name "**Pakistan**".
- **Jinnah** left the Congress in 1920, then returned to League leadership in the 1930s. After the **1937 elections** the League demanded to be the sole voice of Muslims.
- **Lahore Resolution, 23 March 1940** (moved by A. K. Fazlul Huq): "independent states".
- **Direct Action Day, 16 August 1946.** **Partition, 1947.**
- **Hindu Mahasabha** (1915) with Madan Mohan Malaviya. **V. D. Savarkar** was its president (1937–43). **RSS** (1925, **K. B. Hedgewar**, Nagpur).

#CH odisha | Odisha in British India and the freedom struggle | PART A · STATIC
This chapter matters for an Odisha exam. The 2018 paper asked about the Treaty of Deogaon and Gandhi's first visit to Cuttack, and the 2025 paper asked about the Revolt of 1857. Learn Odisha as a line.

@@fig odisha

##Before 1900
- **1803:** after the second Maratha war, the Bhonsle ruler of Nagpur gave up Cuttack, Puri and Balasore by the **Treaty of Deogaon** (17 December 1803). ⟦2018 Q27⟧ Earlier, the **Diwani of Bengal, Bihar and Odisha** had been given in 1765.
- **Khurda and the Paikas.** The Raja of Khurda was the keeper of the Jagannath temple. The British took away the rent-free lands (jagirs) of the **Paikas**, the soldiers of the Raja. The price of salt rose and the Company brought silver coins in place of cowries.
- **Paika Rebellion, 1817.** Led by **Buxi Jagabandhu Bidyadhar Mahapatra Bhramarbar Ray**, the commander of the Paikas. The Khond tribes of Ghumsur helped. They killed British officers and burnt government buildings at Banpur, Khurda and Pipili. The British suppressed it by 1818. Buxi Jagabandhu surrendered in 1825 and died in 1829. The 200th year (2017) was marked, and Odisha asks that it be called the **first war of Indian independence**. ⟨CHECK name of the title⟩
- **Khond revolts:** Ghumsur (1836–37), **Chakra Bisoi** (1846–56), against the British efforts to end the human sacrifice (Meriah). ⟨CHECK dates⟩
- **Sambalpur and Veer Surendra Sai.** Born 23 January 1809. After the death of Raja Narayan Singh in 1849 the British took Sambalpur by the **Doctrine of Lapse**. Surendra Sai fought for 37 years. He joined the **Revolt of 1857** and surrendered in 1862, then arrested again in 1864 and sent to **Asirgarh fort** (Madhya Pradesh), where he died in 1884. His aides included Madho Singh and Kamal Singh. ⟦2025 Q75⟧
- **Famine of 1866 (Na'anka Durbhiksha).** About one-third of the people died. Governor **Cecil Beadon** delayed help. Roads, canals (Orissa Canal, Taladanda), and a rail were built as lessons.
- **Press and language.** **Utkal Dipika (1866)** by **Gourishankar Ray** (Cuttack). Some books name **Bodhadayini (1861, Balasore)** as the first Odia paper ⟨CHECK⟩. **Fakir Mohan Senapati** (b. 1843, Balasore) is called the "**father of Odia prose**". His **Chha Mana Atha Guntha** (1902) is the first Odia novel. **Radhanath Ray** was the Kavi. A debate in the 1860s over whether Odia was a dialect of Bengali was settled by Odia writers and by **John Beames**, who said Odia is a separate language. ⟨CHECK⟩
- **Ravenshaw College** at Cuttack (1868 as a school, a college from 1876).

##The movement for an Odisha province
|Year|Event|
|---|---|
|1903|**Utkal Union Conference (Utkal Sammilani)** at Cuttack. **Madhusudan Das** (Madhubabu, "**Utkal Gaurav**") was its leader. First session was presided over by Maharaja Sriram Chandra Bhanja Deo of Mayurbhanj ⟨CHECK⟩|
|1905|Curzon's partition of Bengal. Odia areas were still scattered across Bengal, Madras and the Central Provinces|
|1912|**Bihar and Orissa** made a separate province. Odisha was joined to Bihar|
|1931|**O'Donnell Committee** recommended a separate Odisha province|
|**1 April 1936**|**Odisha becomes a province** under the **Government of India Act, 1935**. First Governor: **Sir John Austen Hubback**. Capital: Cuttack. This is **Utkal Divas**|
|1937|First elections. **Biswanath Das** formed the first Congress ministry|

>KEY:Madhusudan Das
Born 28 April 1848 at Satyabhamapur, Cuttack. First Odia graduate and first Odia lawyer. Founded the **Utkal Tannery** (1905) and the Orissa Art Ware Works. Leader of the Utkal Sammilani. Died 1934. The 2026 Utkal Divas state function was held at his birthplace.

##Gopabandhu Das and the Satyabadi group
- **Gopabandhu Das** (1877–1928), called "**Utkalmani**". Born at Suando, Puri. Founded the **Satyabadi Bana Vidyalaya** (1909) at Sakhigopal. Started the weekly **The Samaja** (1919), daily from 1927. Founded the **Lokasevak Mandal** (1927). Wrote in jail at Hazaribagh (Bandira Atmakatha). Died 1928.
- His group: **Nilakantha Das, Godavarish Mishra, Krupasindhu Mishra, Harihar Acharya** (the "Panchasakha" of the Satyabadi age).

##Gandhi and the national movement in Odisha
- **Gandhi's first visit to Odisha** was **23 March 1921** at **Cuttack** ⟦2018 Q31⟧. He came again in 1925, 1927, 1928 and 1934. The **Harijan padayatra** of 1934 went from Puri to Bhadrak. ⟨CHECK years⟩
- **Utkal Pradesh Congress Committee** was formed in 1920.
- **Salt Satyagraha 1930** in Odisha was at **Inchudi (Balasore)** under **Gopabandhu Choudhury**, with Rama Devi, **Malati Devi** and Nabakrushna Choudhury, and at Kujang (Cuttack) in 1930.
- **Quit India, 1942.** **Eram firing (28 September 1942)** in Balasore, where police opened fire on a procession and killed many. **Lakshman Naik**, a tribal leader of **Koraput**, led the attack on the Mathili police station and was hanged in 1943.
- **Baji Rout** (Dhenkanal), a 12-year-old boatman, was shot by the police on 11 October 1938 for refusing to ferry them. Called Odisha's youngest martyr.
- **Prajamandal movements** against princely rulers in Dhenkanal, Talcher, Nilagiri, Ranpur and others. **Sarangadhar Das** led the Dhenkanal Prajamandal.

##After 1947
- **Orissa States Agreement (December 1947)** by Patel. About 24 princely states, including **Dhenkanal, Nilgiri, Talcher** and **Ranpur**, merged with Odisha on **1 January 1948**. **Mayurbhanj** merged in 1949. **Seraikela and Kharsawan** went to Bihar. ⟨CHECK numbers⟩
- The new capital **Bhubaneswar** was begun in 1948 (foundation stone laid by Nehru on 13 April 1948).
