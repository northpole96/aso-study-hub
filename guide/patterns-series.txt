#CH start | How to use this sheet | READ FIRST · 1 minute
This is a one-page list of every rule a number or letter series can follow in the ASO paper. It holds the 52 rules used in Series Sets 01 and 02 and the rules that have not come yet. Similar rules sit together in one group, so you learn them as families, not one by one. Each rule has a short example; the answer is in **bold**.

Read one group, then cover the right-hand column and say the rule of each example out loud. When a rule takes you more than ten seconds to see, mark it and come back. The full method, with worked past questions, is in the **Number Series Guide**.

>KEY:The checking order
**Look → gaps → signs → split → ratio → powers → terms before → letters.** The groups below follow the same order.

#CH gaps | Gaps | GROUP 1 · write the differences
Most series are built by adding. Write the difference between each pair of neighbours under the series. The row of gaps is a small series of its own, and it is usually easy.

#CH multiply | Multiplying | GROUP 3 · divide each term by the one before
