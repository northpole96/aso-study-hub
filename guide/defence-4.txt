#CH sindoor | Operation Sindoor and the security events of 2025 | PART B · NEWS
**Operation Sindoor** is the most quoted defence event of the exam window. A question can come from its cause, its date, its name, its weapons or its aftermath.

##The story in order
- **22 April 2025:** terrorists killed 26 civilians at **Baisaran meadow, Pahalgam**, Jammu and Kashmir. India held the **Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance**, closed the Attari border post and cut other ties.
- **7 May 2025:** at about 1.05 am India struck **nine sites** in Pakistan and Pakistan-occupied Kashmir under the name **Operation Sindoor**. The targets named by India included the Jaish-e-Mohammed centre at **Bahawalpur** and the Lashkar-e-Taiba centre at **Muridke**, and sites at Muzaffarabad, Kotli, Bhimber and Sialkot. India said it hit only terrorist camps. "Sindoor" is the red mark worn by married Hindu women, a name chosen to honour the victims. ⟨CHECK number of sites; sources differ⟩
- **7 to 10 May 2025:** four days of fighting with drones, missiles and air attacks. Pakistan called its action **Bunyan-um-Marsoos**.
- **10 May 2025:** the two armies' Directors General of Military Operations (DGMOs) agreed a ceasefire by hotline.
- Daily briefings were given by Foreign Secretary **Vikram Misri**, Colonel **Sofiya Qureshi** and Wing Commander **Vyomika Singh**.
- **28 July 2025: Operation Mahadev.** The three terrorists behind Pahalgam were killed

##What was used
India used BrahMos and other missiles, loitering munitions such as **Harop, Harpy, Nagastra and Sky Striker**, and its air defence: S-400, Akash and the Akashteer control network. The lessons shaped later purchases: more S-400 missiles, anti-drone systems (Akash Tarang, Dharashakti), loitering munitions and the **Shaktibaan** drone regiments (Chapter 21).

##Aftermath in 2026
- The **first anniversary** was marked at the **Joint Commanders' Conference**, Jaipur, 7 and 8 May 2026 (theme "Military Capability in New Domains"). New doctrines were released.
- **Defence Forces Vision 2047** was released in March 2026.
- The **budget of February 2026** was the first since Sindoor and raised defence by about 15%.

>KEY:How India alternates
Most exercises are held **turn by turn**: one year in India, the next in the partner. So if the 2025 edition was abroad, the 2026 edition is usually in India. This gives you a rule to check any place: Dharma Guardian 2025 Japan then 2026 India. Yudh Abhyas 2025 Alaska then 2026 India. SIMBEX 2025 Singapore then 2026 India. KAZIND 2026 in Kazakhstan, so 2027 is in India.

>CHECK:Places the older notes had wrong
Some exam-prep lists place **DUSTLIK 2026 at Aundh, Pune**, and **Yudh Abhyas 2026 in Alaska**. The Army and the US Army say **Yudh Abhyas 2026 was at Auli and Mahajan**, and DUSTLIK-VII (12 to 25 April 2026) was reported in **Uzbekistan**. Trust this table.
