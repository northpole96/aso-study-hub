#CH reforms | Reforms: theatre commands, new Army shape and drones | PART B · NEWS
##Integrated theatre commands
A **theatre command** puts soldiers, ships and planes of a region under one commander. India has none yet, apart from the tri-service Andaman and Nicobar Command and the Strategic Forces Command.
- In **April 2026** three sets of proposals went to the Defence Ministry. The plan reported: a **Northern** command for China, a **Western** command for Pakistan and a **Maritime** command for the Indian Ocean. The Air Force was reported to lead the Western theatre.
- In July 2026 the CDS was to ask the Defence Minister to approve the plan. It also needs the Cabinet Committee on Security.
- In August 2026 the Government told Parliament the report was "under consideration". **No theatre command has been notified as of November 2026.** ⟨CHECK⟩

##The Army's new shape (from 1 July 2026)
|New unit|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**Integrated Battle Groups (IBGs)**|All-arms groups (infantry, armour, artillery, engineers) of more than 5,000 soldiers. Five IBGs and one Fire Support Group were put under 17 Mountain Strike Corps at Panagarh|
|**Rudra brigades**|All-arms brigades|
|**Bhairav battalions**|Light commando battalions. Appeared on Republic Day 2026. One took part in Mountain Guardian|
|**Shaktibaan regiments**|Drone and unmanned units. 15 to 20 planned. Drones of 5 km to 500 km range. Loitering munition tender of about ₹2,000 crore|
|**Divyastra batteries**|Precision-strike units|
|**Shaurya drone squadrons**|Six squadrons placed in tank regiments|
|**Counter-drone grid**|A joint grid to counter enemy drones|

The Army chief's theme for 2026 is "Networking and Data Centricity".

##Drones and new technology
- **Loitering munitions:** Nagastra (Solar Defence), Vijayastra (Solar, announced February 2026), Sky Striker, Harop.
- **Directed energy:** a private firm gave the Army a compact 2 kW laser weapon on an unmanned ground vehicle ⟨CHECK⟩.
- **Quantum:** Military Quantum Mission policy (January 2026). QpiAI's 64-qubit "Kaveri" processor ⟨CHECK⟩.
- **Counter-terror:** the PRAHAAR policy (February 2026).
