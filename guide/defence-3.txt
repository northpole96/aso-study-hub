#CH nuclear | Nuclear policy, space and cyber | PART A · STATIC
##Nuclear tests and doctrine
- **Pokhran-I:** 18 May **1974**, code name **Smiling Buddha**, in the time of Indira Gandhi. Called a "peaceful nuclear explosion".
- **Pokhran-II:** 11 and 13 May **1998**, code name **Operation Shakti**, in the time of Atal Bihari Vajpayee. **National Technology Day** (11 May) recalls it.
- **Draft doctrine:** 1999, by the National Security Advisory Board (K. Subrahmanyam). **Official doctrine:** January 2003, adopted by the Cabinet Committee on Security.
- Main points: **No First Use** (India will not strike first, but will hit back massively if attacked); **credible minimum deterrence**; civil control through the Nuclear Command Authority; the Strategic Forces Command (2003) runs the weapons.
- Triad: land (Agni, Prithvi), air (aircraft), sea (Arihant class submarines).
- India has **not signed** the NPT or the CTBT. It is a member of the **MTCR** (2016), the **Wassenaar Arrangement** (2017) and the **Australia Group** (2018). It is not yet in the Nuclear Suppliers Group.
- In **2008** India and the USA made the civil nuclear deal (123 Agreement). In August 2026 Australia agreed to sell uranium to India ⟨CHECK⟩.
- SIPRI estimates India holds about **190 warheads** (2026 Yearbook) ⟨CHECK⟩.

>WARN:Two Shaktis
**Operation Shakti** = the nuclear tests of 1998. **Mission Shakti** = the anti-satellite test of 2019. A question may put them side by side.

##Space and defence
- **Mission Shakti:** 27 March **2019**. DRDO shot down a satellite in low orbit. India became the **fourth** country after the USA, Russia and China.
- **Defence Space Agency** (2019). **Defence Cyber Agency** (2019).
- **GSAT-7 "Rukmini"** (2013) is the Navy's communication satellite. **GSAT-7A "Angry Bird"** (2018) is the Air Force's. A new **GSAT-7C** was contracted with NSIL for about ₹2,563 crore in October 2026.
- **Space-Based Surveillance:** a plan for over 50 spy satellites, growing to about 150 ⟨CHECK⟩.
- **Sudarshan Chakra Mission:** announced by the Prime Minister on 15 August 2025. It aims to make a national shield against air attack. In 2025 the exam asked about the **Iron Dome** (Israel) and the **Golden Dome** (USA), so India's own shield is the next angle. ⟦2025 Q9⟧
- **Military Quantum Mission Policy Framework:** released in January 2026 by the CDS.
- **Defence Forces Vision 2047:** a roadmap released in March 2026, about eight months after Operation Sindoor.

##Nuclear energy (briefly)
- **SHANTI Act** of 2025 opened the nuclear sector ⟨CHECK⟩. India plans **100 GW** of nuclear power by 2047. At least five small modular reactors are planned by 2033, the first at Tarapur ⟨CHECK⟩.
- The **Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor** at Kalpakkam is reported to have reached first criticality on 6 April 2026 ⟨CHECK⟩.

##Peace operations and rescue operations
- India is one of the **largest troop contributors** to UN peacekeeping. Major Abhilasha Barak (UNIFIL) was named the UN Military Gender Advocate of the Year 2025 ⟨CHECK⟩.
- **Evacuations:** Operation **Raahat** (Yemen, 2015), **Devi Shakti** (Afghanistan, 2021), **Ganga** (Ukraine, 2022), **Kaveri** (Sudan, 2023), **Ajay** (Israel, 2023), **Sindhu** (Iran, 2025).
- **Relief:** **Maitri** (Nepal quake, 2015), **Brahma** (Myanmar quake, 2025), **Sagar Bandhu** (Sri Lanka cyclone, 2025), **Amistad** (Venezuela quake, June 2026 ⟨CHECK⟩).
- **Rescue in the hills:** **Jindagi** (Silkyara tunnel, Uttarakhand, 2023, 41 workers) ⟦2025 Q11⟧. **Him Setu** (North Sikkim landslide, April 2026 ⟨CHECK⟩).

##Siachen
**Siachen** is the highest battlefield in the world. It is a glacier in the eastern **Karakoram** range in Ladakh. India has held the **Saltoro Ridge** since **Operation Meghdoot** (1984).

##Other places
- **Chicken's Neck:** the narrow Siliguri corridor in West Bengal that joins the Northeast to the rest of India.
- **Nyoma airfield** in eastern Ladakh. An IAF IL-76 landed there in 2026 ⟨CHECK⟩. DRDO tested a high-altitude parachute at Nyoma in October 2026.
- **Andaman and Nicobar Islands:** the base of the first tri-service command. **Exercise Dweep Shakti** (24 to 28 March 2026) was its tri-service drill.
- **Haldia** in West Bengal: a new Navy base planned in 2026.

##Heritage and tourism
**Bharat Ranbhoomi Darshan** is a Ministry of Defence initiative that opens battlefields and border posts to visitors. ⟦2025 Q13⟧

- **Quad:** USA, India, Japan, Australia. ⟦2021 Q83⟧ **Malabar** is its naval exercise.

#CH welfare | Welfare, schemes and institutions | PART A · STATIC
##Recruitment and the Agnipath scheme
- **Agnipath** was launched in **June 2022**. Young people (age 17½ to 21) join as **Agniveers** for **four years**. After that up to **25%** are kept for another **15 years** in the regular cadre. The others leave with a one-time "Seva Nidhi" package. Women Agniveers joined all three services.
- **National Defence Academy** entry is through the UPSC exam. The **Combined Defence Services (CDS) exam** feeds the IMA, OTA, Naval and Air Force academies. **AFCAT** is the Air Force's own test. In 2026 the Air Force opened its technical branch to candidates with a **GATE** score ⟨CHECK⟩.
- **Sainik Schools** prepare boys and girls for the NDA. The first was at Satara (1961). **Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC)**, Dehradun, was founded in 1922.
- **National Cadet Corps (NCC):** set up in 1948 under the National Cadet Corps Act. Motto "Unity and Discipline". Three wings: Army, Navy, Air.

##Veterans and pensions
- **One Rank One Pension (OROP):** accepted in 2015. Soldiers of the same rank and service length get the same pension, whenever they retired.
- **ECHS** (Ex-servicemen Contributory Health Scheme), 2003, gives cashless health care to veterans.
- The **Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare** looks after resettlement. **Armed Forces Flag Day** (7 December) raises funds.
- The **Armed Forces Tribunal** (2009) hears service cases of the forces.

##Memorials and heritage
- **National War Memorial**, New Delhi, near India Gate: inaugurated on **25 February 2019**.
- **Bharat Ranbhoomi Darshan:** battlefields and border posts opened to tourists by the Ministry of Defence ⟦2025 Q13⟧.
- **Vijay Durg:** the new name of Fort William, Kolkata ⟦2025 Q14⟧.
- **Gandiv Vijay:** an Army exercise held in Rajasthan ⟦2018 Q12⟧.
- **Cantonment Boards** run the towns of the military stations under the Cantonment Act, 2006.

##Other bodies

|Directorate General of Quality Assurance (DGQA)|Checks the quality of military stores|
|Defence Estates|Looks after defence land|
|National Cadet Corps|Youth training under the Ministry of Defence|
|Armed Forces Medical Services (DGAFMS)|Hospitals of the forces|
|Defence Accounts Department|Pays and audits. Founded in 1747. The 279th annual day was on 1 October 2026|
