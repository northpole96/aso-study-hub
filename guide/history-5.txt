#CH news | History in the news, 2025 and 2026 | PART B · NEWS
The 2025 paper had only a few "news" questions on history, but they were of one kind: **anniversaries, days, renamed places and heritage**. Since the exam is in January 2027, the big commemorations of 2025 and 2026 are the likely source. This chapter lists them with the static fact beside each one, so that a news question can always be answered from the old facts.

##The big commemorations
|Commemoration|Dates|Static fact to pair with it|
|---|---|---|
|**150 years of Vande Mataram**|Year-long, **7 November 2025 to 7 November 2026**. Launched by the Prime Minister at the Indira Gandhi Indoor Stadium, New Delhi, with a stamp, a coin and a portal. Four phases: 7–14 Nov 2025; around 26 Jan 2026; 7–15 Aug 2026; closing week 1–7 Nov 2026|The song was written by **Bankim Chandra Chatterjee** on 7 November 1875 (Akshaya Navami). It appeared in his novel **Anandamath (1882)**. Tagore sang it at the **1896 Calcutta Congress**. The Constituent Assembly gave it the status of **national song** (24 January 1950) next to the national anthem|
|**150th birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel**|From **31 October 2025** (a year-long programme; some reports say two years). **Bharat Parv** was held from 1 to 15 November 2025 ⟨CHECK length⟩|Born 31 October 1875 at Nadiad. **Bardoli (1928)** gave him "Sardar". Presided at **Karachi (1931)**. First Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister. Joined 565 princely states. **Rashtriya Ekta Diwas** is 31 October. **Statue of Unity** (Kevadia, Gujarat; unveiled 31 October 2018)|
|**150th birth anniversary of Birsa Munda**|**15 November 2025**. **Janjatiya Gaurav Varsh** ended with a fortnight (1–15 November 2025)|Born 15 November 1875 at Ulihatu, Khunti (Jharkhand). Led the **Ulgulan (Munda rebellion)** of 1899–1900. Died in Ranchi jail, June 1900. **Chotanagpur Tenancy Act (1908)**. **Janjatiya Gaurav Divas** is 15 November|
|**Centenary of Atal Bihari Vajpayee**|25 December 2024 to 25 December 2025|Prime Minister 1996, 1998–2004 (post-independence)|
|**300th birth anniversary of Ahilyabai Holkar**|31 May 2025|Maratha queen of Indore who rebuilt temples at Varanasi (Kashi Vishwanath), Somnath and Gaya|
|**90 years of Odisha as a province (Utkal Divas)**|**1 April 2026**. A fortnight, "Odia Pakhya", was launched. The state function was held at **Satyabhamapur**, the birthplace of **Madhusudan Das**|Odisha became a separate province on **1 April 1936**. First Governor: **Sir John Hubback**|
|**50 years of the Emergency**|**25 June 2025** (declared in 1975)|The Government made **25 June "Samvidhan Hatya Diwas"** (2024)|
|**107th anniversary of Jallianwala Bagh**|**13 April 2026**|13 April 1919. Dyer. Hunter Committee. Udham Singh killed O'Dwyer (1940)|
|**Parakram Diwas**|23 January. The 129th birth anniversary of **Netaji** in 2026|Bose born 23 January 1897 at Cuttack, Odisha. Provisional Government of Free India, 21 October 1943|

>KEY:Netaji and Odisha
**Subhas Chandra Bose was born at Cuttack, Odisha, on 23 January 1897.** He studied at the Ravenshaw Collegiate School, Cuttack. A question may use a news item on Parakram Diwas and ask where Bose was born.

##Names changed and places
- **Fort William, Kolkata** (the Army's Eastern Command base) was renamed **Vijay Durg**. Its **St George's Gate** became **Shivaji Gate**. ⟦2025 Q14⟧
- **Port Blair** was renamed **Sri Vijaya Puram** (2024). The Andaman islands **Ross**, **Neil** and **Havelock** were renamed **Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Dweep**, **Shaheed Dweep** and **Swaraj Dweep** (2018). Bose had hoisted the flag at Port Blair on 30 December 1943.
- **Rajpath** became **Kartavya Path** (2022). A statue of Netaji stands at India Gate (2022).
- **Ahmednagar** was renamed **Ahilyanagar** ⟨CHECK year⟩. In 2026 **Kerala** was renamed **Keralam** ⟨CHECK⟩.

##Heritage
- **UNESCO World Heritage 2025:** the **Maratha Military Landscapes of India**, twelve forts of the Maratha rulers (Raigad, Rajgad, Shivneri, Pratapgad, Panhala, Salher, Lohgad, Khanderi, Suvarnadurg, Vijaydurg and Sindhudurg in Maharashtra, and Gingee in Tamil Nadu), was inscribed in July 2025 ⟨CHECK count and site number⟩. The **Moidams** of Assam were inscribed in 2024. These sites belong to the earlier history but come as news.
- **Bharat Ranbhoomi Darshan:** an MoD initiative that opens battlefields to tourists ⟦2025 Q13⟧.

##Eleven lists to know by heart
- **Viceroys:** Canning (1858), Lytton (VPA 1878), Ripon (repeal 1882, Ilbert 1883), Curzon (Bengal 1905), Minto (League 1906, 1909), Hardinge (Delhi 1911), Chelmsford (Rowlatt 1919), Irwin (Dandi 1930), Willingdon (Poona 1932), Linlithgow (Quit India 1942), Wavell (Cabinet Mission 1946), Mountbatten (1947).
- **Acts:** 1773 Regulating, 1784 Pitt, 1813 education, 1833 Governor-General of India, 1853 open exam, 1858 Crown, 1861, **1892 election**, **1909 separate electorates**, **1919 dyarchy**, **1935 provincial autonomy**, 1947 Independence.
- **Land systems:** Permanent 1793 (Cornwallis), Ryotwari 1820 (Munro), Mahalwari 1822 (Holt Mackenzie).
- **Education order:** 1813, 1835 Macaulay, 1854 Wood, 1882 Hunter, 1904 Curzon's Act, 1917 Sadler, 1929 Hartog, 1937 Wardha, 1944 Sargent.
- **Press:** 1780 Hicky, 1799 Wellesley, 1835 Metcalfe, 1878 Lytton, 1882 Ripon.
- **Organisations:** Brahmo 1828, Arya 1875, Congress 1885, League 1906, Ghadar 1913, AITUC 1920, CPI 1920/25, Swaraj Party 1923, Labour Swaraj 1925, CSP 1934, Kisan Sabha 1936, Forward Bloc 1939.
- **Congress sessions:** 1885 Bombay, 1906 Calcutta (Swaraj), 1907 Surat (split), 1916 Lucknow (pact), 1917 Besant, 1924 Belgaum (Gandhi), 1925 Kanpur (Sarojini), 1929 Lahore (Purna Swaraj), 1931 Karachi (Patel), 1938 Haripura, 1939 Tripuri.
- **Gandhi:** 1917 Champaran, 1918 Ahmedabad and Kheda, 1919 Rowlatt, 1920 Non-Cooperation, 1930 Dandi (12 March–6 April), 1931 Pact, 1932 Poona, 1942 Quit India (8 August).
- **1857:** Meerut 10 May; leaders Delhi (Bahadur Shah, Bakht Khan), Kanpur (Nana Saheb), Lucknow (Hazrat Mahal), Jhansi (Lakshmibai), Jagdishpur (Kunwar Singh), Faizabad (Ahmadullah), Sambalpur (Surendra Sai); Proclamation 1 Nov 1858.
- **Odisha:** 1803 Deogaon; 1817 Paika (Buxi Jagabandhu); 1866 famine; 1903 Utkal Sammilani; 1936 province; 1938 Baji Rout; 1942 Eram; 1948 merger.
- **Independence:** Cabinet Mission 1946, Direct Action 16 Aug 1946, 3 June 1947, 15 August 1947, Radcliffe, Operation Polo 1948.
