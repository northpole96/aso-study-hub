This is a full mock on the number system alone: fifty questions in forty-five minutes, the pace of the real paper. It covers every type in chapters 7 to 14 of the Arithmetic Guide in the weight the papers give them: divisibility, remainders and successive division, last digits, HCF and LCM in all their word-problem and remainder forms, fractions, number facts and decimals.

Six questions are real past questions word for word (2025 Q56, Q61, Q70 and Q84; 2021 Q53 and Q54). Every other question is new and is not in Number System Set 01. Each answer names its type and the guide chapter that explains it. Press Copy answers at the end and paste the report into the chat for a redo set.
