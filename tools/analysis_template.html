What was asked

Pick a paper. For every topic, and for every family inside the topic, this page counts the questions that came in each year. A family is one kind of question that keeps coming back, such as "days and themes" in General Awareness or "time and work" in Maths. A dark box means many questions that year; a dot means none. Use it to see what the examiner likes, what he has dropped, and which families have not come yet.
