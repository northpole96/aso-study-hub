Time and work, with its cousin pipes and cisterns, was one of the largest families in the 2025 maths section, and 2025 Q53 (extra men to finish on time) is the kind of sum that comes back with new numbers. This short test has 14 questions, one for each of the 11 types on your list (the pairs type has two parts). They are mixed, so you have to spot the type yourself, which is what the exam asks.

One idea solves almost all of them: call the whole job 1, and a person who takes n days does 1/n of it in a day. Each answer names the type, shows the working step by step, gives a shortcut where there is one, and explains the trap options. Keep rough paper ready; no calculator.
