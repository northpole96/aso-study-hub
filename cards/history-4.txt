# history | British Rule and Freedom

## his-red

= aituc
D 1920 | AITUC | The **All India Trade Union Congress** was founded at **Bombay**. It was the first all-India body of workers. **Lala Lajpat Rai** was its first president.
Q In which year and in which city was the All India Trade Union Congress founded?
A 1920, Bombay
Q Which labour leader, named in the 2025 paper, was one of the builders of the AITUC?
A N. M. Joshi
Q Who founded the Ahmedabad Textile Labour Association?
A Mahatma Gandhi
Q Which Act of 1926 gave trade unions a legal standing?
A Trade Union Act

= cpi
Q Who founded the Communist Party of India, and where?
A M. N. Roy, at Tashkent
N The year was 1920. A party inside India was formed later, at Kanpur in 1925.
S The man with many names | He was born Narendranath Bhattacharya in Bengal. In 1915 he left India to find guns for a revolt, and the search took him across the world. In America he took a new name, **M. N. Roy**. In Mexico he helped to start a communist party. In Moscow he argued with Lenin. And in **1920**, at **Tashkent** in Central Asia, he founded the **Communist Party of India** with a few other exiles. The party was born thousands of miles from the country it was named after.

= lsp
Q In which year and in which province was the Labour Swaraj Party formed?
A 1925, Bengal
Q Name the three leaders of the Labour Swaraj Party.
A Muzaffar Ahmed, Qazi Nazrul Islam and Hemanta Kumar Sarkar
N S. A. Dange is the trap. He worked in Bombay and was not in this party.
Q Which famous poet was among the founders of the Labour Swaraj Party?
A Qazi Nazrul Islam

= kanpur
Q In which conspiracy case of 1924 were Dange and Muzaffar Ahmed tried?
A Kanpur Bolshevik Conspiracy Case

= meerut
D 1929 | Meerut Conspiracy Case | In March the government arrested about thirty labour leaders in one sweep, among them **Muzaffar Ahmed, S. A. Dange and P. C. Joshi**, and three Englishmen. The trial dragged on until 1933.
Q In which year did the Meerut Conspiracy Case begin?
A 1929
Q Name three well-known accused in the Meerut Conspiracy Case.
A Muzaffar Ahmed, S. A. Dange and P. C. Joshi
Q The Meerut Conspiracy Case was aimed at which group of people?
A Communist and trade union leaders
F Why Meerut? | The arrested leaders worked in Bombay and Calcutta. In those cities they would have been tried before a **jury**, and a jury might have let them go. So the government held the trial in the small town of **Meerut**, where no jury was needed. The plan failed. The long trial made the accused famous across India.
F Two cases, five years apart | **Kanpur 1924**: a few communists, among them Dange and Muzaffar Ahmed, were tried for plotting against the King. **Meerut 1929**: about thirty labour leaders, with the same two names again, plus P. C. Joshi and three Englishmen. Both cases tried to crush the Left and both made it better known.

= left
Q Who formed the Congress Socialist Party, and in which year?
A Jayaprakash Narayan and Acharya Narendra Dev, 1934
N It worked inside the Congress, not against it.
Q Where and in which year was the All India Kisan Sabha formed, and who was its first president?
A Lucknow, 1936; Swami Sahajanand Saraswati
F The Left in five dates | **1920** AITUC, and the CPI at Tashkent. **1925** Labour Swaraj Party in Bengal. **1929** Meerut Conspiracy Case. **1934** Congress Socialist Party. **1936** All India Kisan Sabha.

= cases
Q What was the Kakori case of 1925 about?
A Revolutionaries robbed a train carrying government money
N Ram Prasad Bismil and Ashfaqulla Khan were hanged for it.
Q The Alipore Conspiracy Case of 1908 was against which leader, and who defended him?
A Aurobindo Ghosh; C. R. Das
Q The Lahore Conspiracy Case was the trial of whom?
A Bhagat Singh and his comrades
N It followed the killing of the police officer Saunders in 1928.
F Do not mix up the cases | **Alipore 1908**: Aurobindo Ghosh, bombs in Bengal. **Kanpur 1924**: communists. **Kakori 1925**: a train robbery by revolutionaries. **Lahore**: Bhagat Singh. **Meerut 1929**: labour leaders. Ask "who was in the dock?" and each case stands apart.
Q Bhagat Singh and B. K. Dutt threw bombs in the Central Assembly in 1929. Which two Bills were they protesting against?
A The Public Safety Bill and the Trade Disputes Bill
N Both Bills were aimed at the labour movement. The bombs were made to make noise, not to kill.

## his-ina

= firstina
D 1942 | The first INA | Singapore fell to Japan in February and thousands of Indian soldiers became prisoners. **Captain Mohan Singh** raised the first **Indian National Army** from them, with Japanese help.
Q Who raised the first Indian National Army, and in which year?
A Captain Mohan Singh, 1942
N Netaji came later. He rebuilt the army in 1943.
Q The first INA was raised from which group of men?
A Indian prisoners of war held by Japan
Q Who led the Indian Independence League?
A Rash Behari Bose
N He had planned the bomb attack on the Viceroy, Lord Hardinge, in 1912, and fled to Japan in 1915.
F Two Boses | **Rash Behari Bose** was the old revolutionary in Japan who led the **Indian Independence League**. **Subhas Chandra Bose** was the Congress leader who escaped from India in 1941. In 1943 the elder Bose handed the League and the army to the younger one. They were not related.

= netaji
Q In which year did Subhas Chandra Bose escape from his house in Calcutta?
A 1941
S Twice elected, once escaped | **Subhas Chandra Bose** was elected Congress president at **Haripura in 1938**, and again at **Tripuri in 1939** against Gandhi's own candidate. The party's leaders would not work with him, so he resigned and formed the **Forward Bloc**. The British shut him in his own house in Calcutta. One night in January **1941** he slipped out in disguise. He crossed Afghanistan and reached Berlin. Two years later a submarine carried him half-way round the world to lead an army.
Q Which party did Subhas Bose form in 1939 after leaving the Congress presidency?
A Forward Bloc
Q How did Subhas Bose travel from Germany to East Asia in 1943?
A By submarine

= azadhind
D 1943 | Provisional Government of Free India | **Subhas Chandra Bose** announced it at **Singapore** on **21 October**. It is also called the Azad Hind government. Japan handed it the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.
Q On which date and in which city did Netaji announce the Provisional Government of Free India?
A 21 October 1943, Singapore
Q Which two war cries did Netaji give the INA?
A "Delhi Chalo" and "Jai Hind"
Q What was the women's unit of the INA called, and who led it?
A Rani of Jhansi Regiment; Lakshmi Sahgal
Q What names did Netaji give to the Andaman and Nicobar Islands?
A Shaheed and Swaraj
Q Complete Netaji's line: "Give me blood, and I will give you ..."
A "... freedom"
Q Up to which two towns in north-east India did the INA advance in 1944?
A Imphal and Kohima

= redfort
D 1945 | Red Fort trials | The British tried three INA officers at the Red Fort in Delhi: **Shah Nawaz Khan, P. K. Sehgal and G. S. Dhillon**. **Bhulabhai Desai** led the defence. The whole country took their side.
Q Name the three INA officers tried at the Red Fort in 1945.
A Shah Nawaz Khan, P. K. Sehgal and G. S. Dhillon
Q Who led the defence in the INA trials at the Red Fort?
A Bhulabhai Desai
N Nehru, Tej Bahadur Sapru and Asaf Ali were also on the defence team.
S Three men in the dock | When the war ended the British wanted to make an example of the INA. They chose three officers and tried them in public at the **Red Fort**. By chance, or by carelessness, the three were a Muslim, a Hindu and a Sikh: **Shah Nawaz Khan, Sehgal and Dhillon**. Nothing could have joined India faster. Crowds shouted their names in every town, and **Nehru** put on his lawyer's gown after many years. The men were found guilty and then quietly set free.
Q In which year did the sailors of the Royal Indian Navy revolt at Bombay?
A 1946

A San Francisco, 1913
N Lala Har Dayal was its moving spirit. Sohan Singh Bhakna was its first president.
Q Who founded the Free India Society in London?
A V. D. Savarkar
Q Who founded India House in London in 1905?
A Shyamji Krishna Varma
Q Who raised an Indian flag at Stuttgart in Germany in 1907?
A Madam Bhikaji Cama
F Two governments in exile | Netaji's government of **1943** at Singapore was not the first. In **1915** a Provisional Government of India was set up at **Kabul**, with **Raja Mahendra Pratap** as President and **Barkatullah** as Prime Minister. If the question says "first", the answer is Kabul.
Q Who was the President of the Provisional Government of India set up at Kabul in 1915?
A Raja Mahendra Pratap
