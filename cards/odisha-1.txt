# odisha | Odisha

## odi-rivers

= hexa
Q Why is Odisha's coastal plain called the Hexadeltaic region?
A Six rivers built it from six deltas that joined
N Asked in 2025.
Q Name the six rivers of the Hexadeltaic region, north to south.
A Subarnarekha, Budhabalanga, Baitarani, Brahmani, Mahanadi, Rushikulya
Q Which river did the most work in building Odisha's coastal plain?
A Mahanadi
N Asked in 2021.
F A coast built from mud | Thousands of years ago much of Odisha's green coast was sea. Six rivers, **Subarnarekha, Budhabalanga, Baitarani, Brahmani, Mahanadi** and **Rushikulya**, carried mud down from the hills and dropped it at their mouths. The six deltas joined into one plain, the **Hexadeltaic region**. The **Mahanadi** did the most work.

= brahmani
Q Which two rivers join to form the Brahmani?
A Sankh and Koel
Q Where do the Sankh and the Koel meet?
A Vedavyas, near Rourkela
Q Which dam stands on the Brahmani?
A Rengali Dam
S A river with no single source | Most rivers begin at one spring. The **Brahmani** begins at a meeting. Two rivers, the **Sankh** and the **Koel**, run down from the Chota Nagpur plateau and join at **Vedavyas**, near Rourkela. Local belief says the sage Vyasa wrote the Mahabharata there. From that spot onward the water has one name, Brahmani. Further down it fills the **Rengali Dam**, then runs to the sea.

## odi-1866

= 1803
Q In which year did the British take Odisha from the Marathas?
A 1803
N Asked in 2018.

= naanka
D 1866 | Na'anka famine | The rains of 1865 failed, help came too late, and about **one-third** of the people of coastal Odisha died. The same year **Utkal Dipika**, the first Odia newspaper, began.
Q What is the famine of 1866 in Odisha called?
A Na'anka famine
Q Why is the 1866 famine called Na'anka?
A It fell in the ninth regnal year of the Gajapati king of Puri
Q About what share of coastal Odisha's people died in the 1866 famine?
A About one-third
Q Who was the Commissioner of Odisha during the 1866 famine?
A T. E. Ravenshaw
S Rice that came too late | In **1865** the rains failed in Odisha. Officials in Calcutta said prices would settle by themselves and sent nothing. When they finally agreed to send rice, the monsoon had come and the rough sea closed the ports. In **1866**, the **Na'anka** year, about one in three people on the coast died. The Commissioner was **T. E. Ravenshaw**, whose name was later given to the college at Cuttack.

= dipika
Q What was the first Odia newspaper, and in which year did it start?
A Utkal Dipika, 1866
Q Who started Utkal Dipika?
A Gourishankar Ray

= sammilani
Q Who founded the Utkal Sammilani in 1903?
A Madhusudan Das
Q On which date did Odisha become a separate province?
A 1 April 1936
F From grief to a province | **1866**: the famine kills a third of the coast, and **Utkal Dipika** begins. Odias ask why their land is split among three provinces. **1903**: **Madhusudan Das** starts the **Utkal Sammilani**. **1 April 1936**: Odisha becomes a separate province.

= surendra
Q Which hero of Sambalpur fought the British during and after 1857?
A Veer Surendra Sai
Q From which jail did rebel sepoys free Surendra Sai in 1857?
A Hazaribagh
Q In which year did Surendra Sai give up arms?
A 1862
Q Where and in which year did Surendra Sai die?
A Asirgarh fort, 1884
S The prisoner set free by the rebels | **Surendra Sai** of **Sambalpur** believed the throne of Sambalpur was his by right, and the British locked him up in **Hazaribagh** jail. In **1857** the rebel sepoys broke the jail open and he walked free. He went home and fought a hill war that went on long after the great Revolt had ended. He laid down arms in **1862**, was arrested again, and died a prisoner in **Asirgarh fort** in **1884**.

= heroes
Q Who led the Paika Rebellion of 1817 in Khurda?
A Buxi Jagabandhu
Q Which priest of Puri helped the rebels of 1857?
A Chakhi Khuntia
Q Which Koraput hero was hanged in 1943 for the Quit India Movement?
A Laxman Naik
F Four heroes, four dates | **Buxi Jagabandhu**: Paika Rebellion, Khurda, **1817**. **Chakhi Khuntia**: Puri priest who helped the rebels of **1857**. **Surendra Sai**: Sambalpur, **1857 to 1862**. **Laxman Naik**: Koraput, hanged in **1943** for Quit India. The examiner swaps the names and the places.

## odi-wild

= similipal
Q When did Similipal become a national park?
A April 2025
Q Similipal is Odisha's which national park, and India's which?
A Odisha's second, India's 107th
Q What is Odisha's first national park?
A Bhitarkanika
N Declared in 1998.
Q From which tree does Similipal get its name?
A Simul, the red silk cotton tree
D 2025 | Similipal National Park | In **April**, after forty-five years of waiting since it was first proposed in 1980, Similipal became **Odisha's second** national park and **India's 107th**, with about **846 sq km**.

= blacktiger
Q What are the near-black tigers of Similipal called?
A Melanistic tigers
Q Name Odisha's two tiger reserves.
A Similipal and Satkosia
Q Besides a tiger reserve, what UNESCO label does Similipal carry?
A Biosphere reserve
S The tiger that looks black | In the hills of **Mayurbhanj** lives a tiger with stripes so thick and close that the whole animal looks nearly black. It is a **melanistic tiger**, and the wild ones are found almost only in **Similipal**. The forest had waited since 1980 to become a national park. The order finally came in **April 2025**, making Similipal Odisha's second national park, after Bhitarkanika.

= ramsar
Q How many Ramsar wetlands does Odisha have?
A Six
Q Name Odisha's six Ramsar wetlands.
A Chilika, Bhitarkanika, Satkosia, Tampara, Hirakud, Ansupa

Six Ramsar sites: **Chilika, Bhitarkanika, Satkosia, Tampara, Hirakud, Ansupa**.
