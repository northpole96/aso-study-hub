# defence | Defence

## def-chiefs

= cds
Q Who became India's third Chief of Defence Staff on 31 May 2026?
A General N. S. Raja Subramani
N He came after Bipin Rawat (the first) and Anil Chauhan (the second).
Q Who was India's first Chief of Defence Staff?
A General Bipin Rawat
Q In which year was the post of Chief of Defence Staff created?
A 2019
N It was made so that the Army, Navy and Air Force plan and fight together.
Q What is the main job given to the new Chief of Defence Staff?
A Setting up joint theatre commands
F Why a CDS at all | Before **2019** each force planned on its own. The **Chief of Defence Staff** sits above the three chiefs' level of planning so that they buy, train and fight together. The big unfinished job is the **theatre command**: one commander for one region, with soldiers, ships and planes all under him.

= chiefs4
F Four chairs in five months | **31 May 2026**: **Raja Subramani** becomes CDS and **Krishna Swaminathan** Navy chief. **30 June**: **Dhiraj Seth** takes the Army. **31 October**: **Ashutosh Dixit** takes the Air Force. Remember the order of the months: May for two, June for the Army, October for the Air Force.
S Every door, a new name | In most years one service chief retires and the others carry on. **2026** was different. Between May and October all four top chairs of the forces changed hands. On a single day, **31 May**, a new **Chief of Defence Staff** and a new **Navy chief** walked in together. The Army followed a month later and the Air Force in the autumn. For an examiner who asked "the present Chief of Air Staff" in 2021, it was a year full of fresh questions.

= navycaptain
Q A Captain in the Navy is equal to which rank in the Army?
A Colonel
N The trap: a Navy Captain is far senior to an Army Captain.
Q A Captain in the Navy is equal to which rank in the Air Force?
A Group Captain
Q An Army Colonel is equal to which rank in the Navy?
A Captain
F The Captain trap | The word **Captain** means two very different ranks. In the **Army** a Captain is a young officer, just above a Lieutenant. In the **Navy** a Captain is equal to an Army **Colonel** and an Air Force **Group Captain**. So when a question says "Captain", first ask: which service?

A Australia

A India, USA, Japan and Australia

= milan
Q How is MILAN different from Malabar or Varuna?
A It is multilateral: many navies take part
N It is hosted by the Indian Navy every two years.
Q Where was MILAN first held, in 1995?
A Andaman and Nicobar Islands
Q In which city is MILAN now held?
A Visakhapatnam
N Home of the Eastern Naval Command.
Q Which event was held at Visakhapatnam along with MILAN in February 2026?
A International Fleet Review ⟨CHECK⟩
S A meeting, not a match | In **1995** the Indian Navy invited a few nearby navies to the **Andaman and Nicobar Islands**. It was not a drill against one partner. It was a get-together of many, and it was called **MILAN**, "meeting". It grew every two years. In time it moved to **Visakhapatnam**, the home of the Eastern Naval Command, and in **February 2026** it was held there along with the International Fleet Review ⟨CHECK⟩. When an option says MILAN is with one country, it is wrong.

## def-missiles

= agni
Q What type of missile is Agni?
A Surface-to-surface ballistic missile
Q What is the range of Agni-5?
A More than 5,000 km
Q Which is the short-range surface-to-surface ballistic missile of the same kind as Agni?
A Prithvi
Q Who is called the Missile Woman of India?
A Dr Tessy Thomas
N She led the Agni projects. Asked in 2021.
F What "ballistic" means | A **ballistic** missile is pushed up by its rocket and then falls on its target like a thrown stone, rising very high on the way. **Agni** and **Prithvi** are ballistic. A **cruise** missile is different: it flies low and level, like a plane with no pilot, all the way.

= pralay
Q What type of missile is Pralay?
A Surface-to-surface, quasi-ballistic missile
N The 2025 paper called it an air-to-surface cruise missile. That was false.
Q What is the range of the Pralay missile?
A About 150 to 500 km
Q Why is Pralay called quasi-ballistic?
A It can change its path in flight

= brahmos
Q Which two countries built BrahMos together?
A India and Russia
Q Which two rivers give BrahMos its name?
A Brahmaputra and Moskva
Q What type of missile is BrahMos?
A Supersonic cruise missile
N Supersonic means faster than sound.
Q What is the name of India's subsonic cruise missile?
A Nirbhay
S Two rivers in one name | When India and Russia agreed to build a missile together, they needed a name that belonged to both. They took the **Brahmaputra**, which flows through Assam, and the **Moskva**, which flows through Moscow. **BrahMos** flies low like a plane but faster than sound, which makes it a **supersonic cruise missile**. It can be fired from land, ships, submarines and aircraft.

= akash
Q What type of missile is Akash?
A Surface-to-air
Q What type of missile is Astra?
A Air-to-air
Q What is the job of the Nag missile?
A Destroying tanks (anti-tank)
Q From which country did India buy the S-400 air defence system?
A Russia
N Asked in 2021.
F Start and target | Name each missile by where it starts and what it hits. **Agni**: land to land. **Akash**: ground to air, at enemy planes. **Astra**: air to air, one plane at another. **Nag**: at tanks. The examiner changes one of these words, so check both halves.

## def-borders

= mha
Q Under which ministry do the Central Armed Police Forces work?
A Ministry of Home Affairs
N The examiner's favourite false statement puts them under Defence.
Q Which border force is under the Ministry of Defence, not Home Affairs?
A Indian Coast Guard
Q In which year was the Indian Coast Guard set up?
A 1978
Q What is SAREX, run by the Coast Guard?
A National search and rescue exercise at sea
N It was held off Paradip in 2026.
F The one that breaks the rule | BSF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles, CRPF and CISF all work under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**. The **Indian Coast Guard**, set up in **1978**, is the odd one out: it is under the **Ministry of Defence**. If a statement puts the BSF under Defence, it is false.

= bsf
D 1965 | Border Security Force raised | After the war with Pakistan, India raised the **BSF** on **1 December 1965**. It guards the borders with **Pakistan and Bangladesh** and works under the Home Ministry.
Q On which date was the Border Security Force raised?
A 1 December 1965
Q The BSF guards India's borders with which two countries?
A Pakistan and Bangladesh
Q Which force guards the India–Bangladesh border?
A Border Security Force

= itbp

A Indo-Tibetan Border Police
Q After which war was the ITBP raised?
A The 1962 war with China
N The 2025 paper had three statements on the ITBP.
Q Which force guards the open borders with Nepal and Bhutan?
A Sashastra Seema Bal
N Raised in 1963.

= assamrifles
Q Which is the oldest of the Central Armed Police Forces?
A Assam Rifles
N Raised in 1835.
Q Which force guards the India–Myanmar border?
A Assam Rifles
Q In which year was the Assam Rifles raised?
A 1835

= crpf
Q In which year did the Central Reserve Police Force begin?
A 1939
Q Which force, raised in 1969, protects airports and big industries?
A Central Industrial Security Force
Q Which force is known as the black cat commandos?
A National Security Guard
N Raised in 1984.
F One border, one force | **Pakistan** and **Bangladesh**: BSF. **China**: ITBP. **Nepal** and **Bhutan**: SSB. **Myanmar**: Assam Rifles. Inside the country: **CRPF**. Airports, the Delhi Metro and factories: **CISF**. Each force was born from an event: 1962 gave the ITBP, 1965 gave the BSF.

= pvc
Q Who received the first Param Vir Chakra?
A Major Somnath Sharma
N In 1947, in Kashmir.
Q Who is the only Air Force officer to receive the Param Vir Chakra?
A Flying Officer Nirmal Jit Singh Sekhon
N For the 1971 war. Asked in 2021.
S Alone in the sky | In December **1971** Pakistani jets attacked the airfield at **Srinagar**. A young pilot, **Flying Officer Nirmal Jit Singh Sekhon**, took off under fire and fought several enemy aircraft at once. He was shot down and killed. He was given the **Param Vir Chakra**, and he is still the **only Air Force officer** to receive it.

= opsall
F A year of names | **Brahma**: earthquake, Myanmar. **Sindoor**: strikes after Pahalgam. **Sindhu**: students home from Iran. **Sagar Bandhu**: cyclone relief, Sri Lanka. **Urja Suraksha**: oil and gas ships escorted. Most are rescue and relief, not war, and the examiner likes exactly these.
S Friend across the sea | Late in **2025** a cyclone named **Ditwah** crossed **Sri Lanka**. Rivers burst their banks and villages went under water. India's ships and aircraft were among the first to arrive, carrying food, medicine and rescue teams. The mission was called **Operation Sagar Bandhu**, which means "friend across the sea". It was the fourth relief operation of a busy year that had begun with **Operation Brahma** in earthquake-hit Myanmar.
