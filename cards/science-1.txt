= tbday
Q On which date is World TB Day?
A 24 March
N Asked in 2025.
Q Why is World TB Day held on 24 March?
A Robert Koch announced the TB germ on that day in 1882
S The germ found on 24 March | In the 1880s tuberculosis killed one in seven people in Europe, and nobody knew why. A German doctor, **Robert Koch**, spent months staining and growing the tissue of patients until he saw a thin rod-shaped germ. On **24 March 1882** he announced it in Berlin: **tuberculosis is caused by a bacterium**. A hundred years later, that date became **World TB Day**.

A 12 January 2026
Q Which satellite was lost on PSLV-C62?
A EOS-N1, named Anvesha
N Built for the DRDO to watch the seas.
Q In which stage did PSLV-C62 fail?
A Third stage, again
S The workhorse stumbles | For thirty years the **PSLV** was ISRO's workhorse, the rocket that almost never failed. On **18 May 2025** **PSLV-C61** lifted off with the earth-watching satellite **EOS-09**. Its third stage lost pressure, and the satellite was lost. Eight months later, on **12 January 2026**, **PSLV-C62** tried again with **Anvesha**. Once more the trouble came in the **third stage**. Two failures in a row, in the same place.

= mof
Q For what did the 2025 Chemistry Nobel go?
A Metal–organic frameworks
Q Who won the 2025 Chemistry Nobel?
A Susumu Kitagawa, Richard Robson and Omar Yaghi
Q What can metal–organic frameworks do with their tiny empty rooms?
A Trap gases, store hydrogen or pull water from desert air
S A crystal full of rooms | Most crystals are packed solid. In the late 1980s **Richard Robson** built one that was mostly empty: metal joints linked by carbon rods, like scaffolding, with tiny rooms inside. **Susumu Kitagawa** showed gases could flow in and out, and **Omar Yaghi** made the frameworks strong and stable. One gram can hold a huge inner surface. Today such crystals can pull drinking water out of **desert air**. In **2025** the three shared the **Chemistry Nobel**.
