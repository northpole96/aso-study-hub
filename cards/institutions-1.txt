# institutions | Institutions

## ins-un

= un
Q On which date was the United Nations founded?
A 24 October 1945
Q How many members does the United Nations have?
A 193

= unsc
Q How many seats does the UN Security Council have?
A 15
Q Name the five permanent members of the Security Council.
A USA, Russia, China, United Kingdom and France
N Each holds a veto.
Q How long is the term of a non-permanent member of the Security Council?
A Two years
N Asked in 2021.
Q How many non-permanent members are elected each year?
A Five
F Five fixed, ten elected | The **Security Council** has **15** seats. **Five** never change and carry a veto: **USA, Russia, China, UK, France**. The other **ten** are elected for **two years**, **five each year**, so half the elected seats change every January.

= unsc2027
Q Name the five countries elected to the Security Council for 2027–28.
A Austria, Kyrgyzstan, Portugal, Trinidad and Tobago, Zimbabwe
Q Which country won a Security Council seat for the first time in June 2026?
A Kyrgyzstan
N It beat the Philippines.
Q Which big European country lost the Security Council election of June 2026?
A Germany
S A first seat for Kyrgyzstan | In **June 2026** the General Assembly voted for five new members of the Security Council, for **2027–28**. Most results were expected. Two were not. **Kyrgyzstan**, a small mountain country in Central Asia, won a seat **for the first time**, beating the Philippines. And **Germany**, one of the richest countries in the world, **lost**. The other winners were **Austria, Portugal, Trinidad and Tobago** and **Zimbabwe**.

= sco2026
Q Where was the SCO summit held in 2026?
A Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan
N The club's 25th year.
Q Where was the SCO summit held in 2025?
A Tianjin, China
F Shanghai is the name, not the office | The **SCO** was founded in **Shanghai in 2001**, but its secretariat is in **Beijing** and its anti-terror office in **Tashkent**. The **BRICS bank** is the one in **Shanghai**. Summits: **Tianjin 2025**, **Bishkek 2026**.

= g20
Q Which countries chaired the G20 in 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026?
A India, Brazil, South Africa, USA
Q At which G20 summit did the African Union become a member?
A New Delhi, 2023
Q Where was the first G20 summit in Africa held?
A Johannesburg, 2025
Q What is the G20's group of the last, present and next chairs called?
A The troika
N The G20 has no permanent office.
S The chair that moves | The **G20** has no headquarters and no permanent staff. Each year one member takes the chair and hosts the summit. In **2023** it was **India**, and at **New Delhi** the **African Union** joined as a member. In **2024** the chair passed to **Brazil**, and in **2025** to **South Africa**, which held the first G20 summit on African soil at **Johannesburg**. In **2026** it went to the **United States**.
