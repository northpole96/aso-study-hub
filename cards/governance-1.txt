# governance | Governance and Civil Service

## gov-frame

= macaulay
Q Which Act of 1853 ended the giving of Company jobs as favours?
A Charter Act of 1853
Q Which committee of 1854 designed the open civil service exam?
A Macaulay Committee
Q Why could few Indians take the early civil service exam?
A It was held only in London

= commissions
Q Which commission of 1886 split the service into Imperial, Provincial and Subordinate branches?
A Aitchison Commission
Q In which year was the Islington Commission set up?
A 1912
Q Put in order: Lee, Macaulay, Islington, Aitchison.
A Macaulay, Aitchison, Islington, Lee
F Four bodies in order | **Macaulay 1854**: an open exam. **Aitchison 1886**: three branches, Imperial, Provincial, Subordinate. **Islington 1912**: a fresh look. **Lee 1923**: a Public Service Commission in India, which opened in **1926**. The years climb by about thirty, then twenty-six, then eleven.

= lee
Q What is the full name of the Lee Commission?
A Royal Commission on the Superior Civil Services
N Asked in 2025.
Q In which year was the Lee Commission set up?
A 1923
Q In which year did India's first Public Service Commission open?
A 1926
D 1926 | The first Public Service Commission | Acting on the **Lee Commission** of **1923**, the British opened India's first **Public Service Commission**. Under the Constitution it became the **UPSC**.

= steel
Q Who called the Indian civil service the "steel frame"?
A Lloyd George
N The British Prime Minister. Sardar Patel later kept the frame and gave it a new purpose.
S The frame that stayed | In 1922 the British Prime Minister, **Lloyd George**, told Parliament that the Indian civil service was the **steel frame** holding the whole empire up. Twenty-five years later, many Indian leaders wanted to pull it down as a tool of foreign rule. **Sardar Patel** disagreed. He told the Constituent Assembly that without a united, protected service, the new country would fall apart. So the frame stayed, and the Constitution gave its officers a shield in **Article 311**.

= art311
Q Under Article 311, who cannot dismiss a civil servant?
A Any authority lower than the one that appointed him
Q Under Article 311, what must happen before a civil servant is dismissed?
A An inquiry where he can defend himself
Q Which Article says an officer serves at the pleasure of the President or Governor?
A Article 310
Q Which Article lets the government make service rules?
A Article 309
F Pleasure, then shield | **Article 309**: the government makes service rules. **Article 310**: the officer serves **at the pleasure** of the President or Governor. **Article 311** limits that pleasure with two shields: **no dismissal by a lower authority**, and **no dismissal without an inquiry**.

= art312
Q Which Article lets Parliament create a new All India Service?
A Article 312
N Asked in 2021 and 2025.
Q What must the Rajya Sabha do before a new All India Service is created?
A Pass a resolution by a two-thirds vote

A 1959
Q Under which department does the LBSNAA work?
A Department of Personnel and Training
N Not the Home Ministry. The police academy at Hyderabad is the one under the Home Ministry.

= sevottam
Q What is Sevottam?
A A quality management system for public services
N Asked in 2025.
Q Which department built Sevottam, and in which year?
A Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances, 2006
Q How many modules does Sevottam have?
A Three
Q Name the three modules of Sevottam.
A Citizen's Charter, Public Grievance Redress, Service Delivery Capability
N Nothing in it is about staff pay.
F Seva plus uttam | **Sevottam** joins "seva" and "uttam": excellent service. Made by **DARPG** in **2006**, it is a **quality management system** with three modules: the **Citizen's Charter**, **Grievance Redress** and **Service Delivery Capability**. If an option names a fourth, such as pay or training, it is the fake.

= arc2
Q What was the title of the Second ARC's first report?
A Right to Information: Master Key to Good Governance
N Asked in 2021.
Q What was the Second ARC's twelfth report called?
A Citizen Centric Administration
Q What is Odisha's Mo Sarkar?
A Officers phone citizens to ask how they were treated
N Begun in 2019.
S The phone call from the office | Most people leave a government office and never hear from it again. In **2019** Odisha tried something new. Under **Mo Sarkar**, "my government", officers pick citizens who visited a police station or a hospital and **phone them** to ask how they were treated. A rude officer could find his name in a report. It was a simple idea, built on the old promise of the **Citizen's Charter**: the citizen at the counter should be heard.

A Central Vigilance Commission

= cvc
D 1964 | Central Vigilance Commission | Set up on the advice of the **Santhanam Committee**, the CVC stood for decades on a government order alone. A law gave it **statutory status in 2003**.
Q In which year was the Central Vigilance Commission set up?
A 1964
Q In which year did the CVC get statutory status?
A 2003

= lokpalword
Q Who coined the word "Lokpal", and in which year?
A L. M. Singhvi, 1963
Q Which body first asked for a Lokpal at the Centre and Lokayuktas in the States?
A First Administrative Reforms Commission, 1966
N It was led at first by Morarji Desai.

= lokpalact
D 2013 | Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act | After the public protest of **2011** and fifty years of failed bills, Parliament passed the Lokpal law. It came into force in **January 2014**.
Q In which year was the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act passed?
A 2013
Q How many Members can the Lokpal have besides its Chairperson?
A Not more than eight
Q What share of Lokpal Members must be judicial Members?
A Half
Q Can the Lokpal look into the Prime Minister?
A Yes, with limits
N It cannot touch foreign relations, security, public order, atomic energy or space.

= lokpalchair
Q Who was the first Chairperson of the Lokpal?
A Justice Pinaki Chandra Ghose
N Appointed in 2019.
Q Who is the present Chairperson of the Lokpal?
A Justice A. M. Khanwilkar
F Fifty years for one word | **1962**: the **Santhanam Committee**. **1963**: **L. M. Singhvi** coins "Lokpal". **1964**: the **CVC**. **1966**: the first ARC asks for a Lokpal. **2013**: the Act at last. **2019**: the first Lokpal, **Justice P. C. Ghose**. The idea took half a century to reach a chair.

## gov-village

= art40
Q Which Article asks the State to organise village panchayats?
A Article 40
N It is a Directive Principle.

= brmehta
D 1957 | Balwant Rai Mehta Committee | It found that village schemes failed because the people had no say. It proposed **three tiers**: **village panchayat, block samiti, district parishad**.
Q Which committee of 1957 proposed three-tier Panchayati Raj?
A Balwant Rai Mehta Committee
Q Name the three tiers proposed by the Balwant Rai Mehta Committee.
A Village panchayat, block samiti, district parishad
Q Where did Nehru open the first Panchayati Raj system on 2 October 1959?
A Nagaur, Rajasthan
Q Does the Balwant Rai Mehta Committee belong among civil service committees?
A No; it is a Panchayati Raj committee
N The 2025 paper wrongly listed it with civil service committees.
S A Gandhi Jayanti in Nagaur | On **2 October 1959**, Gandhi's birthday, **Jawaharlal Nehru** stood in **Nagaur** in the Rajasthan desert and opened India's first three-tier Panchayati Raj. The plan came from the **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee**, which had found that village schemes failed because villagers had no say. Gandhi had dreamed of every village as a small republic. That morning, the dream got its first office.

= ashok
Q Which committee of 1977 suggested a two-tier Panchayati Raj?
A Ashok Mehta Committee

= gvkrao
Q Which committee called Panchayati Raj "grass without roots"?
A G. V. K. Rao Committee, 1985
Q Which unit did the G. V. K. Rao Committee want as the main unit of planning?
A The district

= singhvi86
Q Which committee of 1986 asked for constitutional status for Panchayats?
A L. M. Singhvi Committee
N The same Singhvi who coined the word "Lokpal".
F Four committees in order | **Balwant Rai Mehta 1957**: three tiers. **Ashok Mehta 1977**: two tiers. **G. V. K. Rao 1985**: **"grass without roots"**, make the district the unit. **L. M. Singhvi 1986**: give Panchayats **constitutional status**. The last wish came true with the **73rd Amendment, 1992**.

= amend73
Q Which Amendment gave Panchayats constitutional status?
A 73rd Amendment, 1992
Q Which Schedule lists the subjects of Panchayats, and how many?
A Eleventh Schedule, 29 subjects
Q What share of Panchayat seats is reserved for women under the 73rd Amendment?
A One-third
Q Which Part of the Constitution deals with Panchayats?
A Part IX
Q Which Amendment did for towns what the 73rd did for villages?
A 74th Amendment
N It added the Twelfth Schedule with 18 subjects.
D 1992 | 73rd and 74th Amendments | Panchayats and town bodies got **constitutional status**. Elections every five years became compulsory, **one-third** of seats went to women, and **State Election Commissions** and **State Finance Commissions** were created.
