## new-unesco

= sarnath
Q Which site became India's 45th World Heritage Site in July 2026?
A Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath
Q In which city was Sarnath added to the World Heritage List?
A Busan, South Korea
Q What did the Buddha do at Sarnath?
A Gave his first sermon
N Its lion pillar gave India its national emblem.
D 2026 | Sarnath on the World Heritage List | In **July**, at **Busan**, UNESCO added the **Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath** as India's **45th** World Heritage Site. Here the Buddha gave his **first sermon**, and here stood the **lion pillar** of the national emblem.

A Documentary heritage: manuscripts and records
N Asked in 2025.

## new-vande

= bankim
Q Who wrote Vande Mataram?
A Bankim Chandra Chatterjee
Q On which date was Vande Mataram written?
A 7 November 1875
Q In which novel did Vande Mataram appear, and in which year?
A Anandamath, 1882
Q What do the words "Vande Mataram" mean?
A Mother, I bow to thee
D 1875 | Vande Mataram is written | On **7 November**, **Bankim Chandra Chatterjee**, a deputy magistrate in Bengal, wrote a poem to the motherland. In **1882** he placed it in his novel **Anandamath**.

= sung
Q Who first sang Vande Mataram in public, and where?
A Rabindranath Tagore, at the 1896 Calcutta Congress
Q In which movement did Vande Mataram become the slogan?
A Swadeshi movement, 1905
S The cry they could not ban | In **1905** Lord **Curzon** divided Bengal. Crowds poured into the streets of Calcutta, and the words they shouted were two: **Vande Mataram**. The British banned the cry in public. It only grew louder, and it became the slogan of the **Swadeshi** movement and the name of a newspaper. The poem had been written thirty years earlier by **Bankim Chandra**, and first sung in public by **Tagore** in **1896**.

= national
Q On which date did Vande Mataram become the National Song?
A 24 January 1950
Q Who wrote Jana Gana Mana, the National Anthem?
A Rabindranath Tagore
Q Which country's anthem, Amar Sonar Bangla, did Tagore write?
A Bangladesh
F Two poets, two songs | **Bankim Chandra**: **Vande Mataram**, the **National Song**. **Tagore**: **Jana Gana Mana**, the **National Anthem**, and Bangladesh's **Amar Sonar Bangla** too. On **24 January 1950** the Constituent Assembly gave the song equal honour with the anthem.
