Chapter 00 · ବନାନ · ପ୍ରତିଶବ୍ଦ · ବିପରୀତ · 98 QUESTIONS

Start here: the word block of the Odia paper

In the Odia paper, one block does not need grammar at all. It needs only words. The block has three parts: ଶୁଦ୍ଧ ବନାନ (the correct spelling, "shuddhi"), ପ୍ରତିଶବ୍ଦ (synonym: a word with the same meaning) and ବିପରୀତ ଶବ୍ଦ (antonym: a word with the opposite meaning). In the three papers of 2018, 2021 and 2025 the examiner asked 98 such questions: 33 on spelling, 32 on synonyms and 33 on antonyms. In 2025 the paper put them first and gave exactly 10 + 10 + 10: Q1–10 spelling, Q11–20 antonyms, Q21–30 synonyms. That is 30 marks out of 100 for a block you can learn by heart.

This guide does two jobs. First, it shows how the examiner chooses a word, so you can see the pattern. Second, it gives a "likely next" list for each part: words the examiner has not asked yet, but that sit right beside the words he did ask. These lists are our prediction. Nobody can know the exact words, but the examiner has a habit of repeating and of moving to the neighbour word, so the lists are a good bet.

0.2What the examiner repeats

The examiner uses the same word again and again, or uses a word next to it:

  • ଅନିବାର୍ଯ୍ୟ was asked word for word in 2018 and again in 2021 2018 Q79 2021 Q1.
  • ନିଅଣ୍ଟ (shortage) and its opposite ବଳକା (what is left over) was asked in 2018 and again in 2021 2018 Q69 2021 Q28.
  • ମେଘ (cloud) was a synonym question in 2018 (answer ଜୀମୂତ) and again in 2025 (answer ଜଳଦ) 2018 Q90 2025 Q27. The word was the same; the answer was the neighbour.
  • ସାପ (snake) came in 2018 (answer ଅହି) and again in 2025 (answer ଉରଗ) 2018 Q89 2025 Q22.
  • ଦନୁଜ (demon) was asked in 2018, 2021 and 2025 in three different ways 2018 Q82 2021 Q14 2025 Q25.

So the plan is simple: learn the whole family of a word, not one answer. If you know all the words for "cloud", it does not matter which one the question prints.

The best order to learn

1. Spelling rules (chapter 1) and the spelling list (chapter 2): the same 10 trap types come again and again.

2. Synonyms by family: sky and weather, land and water, animals, gods and people (chapters 3 and 4).

3. Antonyms in pairs: learn both words together (chapters 5 and 6).

4. Last-week sheet (chapter 7).

The key is a book's key, not OPSC's

OPSC has not published an answer key that we could fetch. Every answer in this guide is the key from the Udyam IAS book of past papers, checked against a standard dictionary where we could. Where keys and grammar disagree, the page shows a CHECK tag. Our "likely next" words are our own prediction; their spellings follow the standard Odia dictionary form of Sanskrit words (the ତତ୍ସମ words), and where two forms are in use we say so.

How to use this guide

Read one chapter. Cover the answers of the Past questions tables and try each one. Then open the Check yourself boxes. Spend most time on chapters 2, 4 and 6: those are the lists.

Chapter 01 · ଶୁଦ୍ଧ ବନାନ · THE RULES

1. Spelling: the ten traps

In a spelling question you get four spellings of one word. One is right. The other three are the same word with a small mistake. The mistakes are not random: in the 33 questions of three years they fall into ten kinds. If you know the ten kinds, you can see the mistake in each wrong option.

A word of comfort first. Most words in these questions are ତତ୍ସମ (tatsama): Sanskrit words used in Odia without change. They follow Sanskrit spelling. So the safest rule is: when two spellings look possible, pick the one that is closest to the Sanskrit word.

1.1Trap 1: short or long ି ୀ ୁ ୂ

Odia has two "i" signs (ି short, ୀ long) and two "u" signs (ୁ short, ୂ long). The question often swaps one for the other. Learn this: most words that end in ି are short (the word is Sanskrit, ending in short i): ତ୍ରୁଟି, ଶକ୍ତି, ପ୍ରକୃତି. Words that name a female person or a river end in ୀ: ଭଗିନୀ, ନଦୀ, ଲକ୍ଷ୍ମୀ. And the first part of a word often has the short sign: ଗରିମା, ପରିଷଦ.

1.2Trap 2: ଶ, ଷ or ସ

Odia has three "s" letters. They sound nearly the same, so the examiner uses all three as bait. The word has only one right choice, and it comes from Sanskrit. Memory help: ଷ is used mostly with ଟ ଠ ଣ କ ପ after it (ବିଷଣ୍ଣ, ଶ୍ରେଷ୍ଠ, ନିଷ୍କଳ, ଦୁଷ୍ପ୍ରାପ୍ୟ), and in ଭୂଷଣ, ପରିଷଦ, ପୁରସ୍କାର each letter has to be learned as it is.

  • ପରିଷଦ (council) 2025 Q1: ଷ, not ଶ or ସ.
  • ପୁରସ୍କାର (prize) 2018 Q68: ସ୍କ with ସ, not ଷ୍କ or ଶ୍କ.
  • ବିଷଣ୍ଣ (sad) 2018 Q72: ଷ୍ଣ.
  • ଅବସନ୍ନ (tired, hopeless) 2025 Q2 CHECK : the book could not read the scan clearly; the option with ସ and ନ୍ନ is the standard spelling.
  • ପର୍ଯ୍ୟବସିତ (ended up as) 2018 Q80: ସ, not ଶ or ଷ.
  • ଶଶିଭୂଷଣ 2025 Q3: first two letters ଶଶି, then ଭୂଷଣ.

1.3Trap 3: ଣ or ନ

The rule is old and simple. After a letter ର, ଷ or ଋ in the same word, a ନ turns into ଣ, unless a letter of the "ch, j, t, d" groups sits in between. So ପ୍ରାଣ, ପ୍ରଣାମ, ଭୂଷଣ, କାରଣ, ଲକ୍ଷଣ, ବ୍ରାହ୍ମଣ all have ଣ. But ପ୍ରୟୋଜନ has ନ (the ଜ in the middle breaks the rule). And in a joined word the ନ stays: ଦୁର୍ନୀତି, ଦୁର୍ନାମ (the word ନୀତି and ନାମ were already complete).

  • ଉତ୍ତରାୟଣ (the sun's northward path) 2018 Q73: ର୍... ୟ... ଣ, so ଣ. The wrong options had ନ.
  • ବିଷଣ୍ଣ 2018 Q72 and ଅକ୍ଷୁଣ୍ଣ (not broken) 2018 Q75: ଣ after ଷ.

1.4Trap 4: ର୍ before a joined letter

Many words have a ର୍ (half r) that sits on top of the next letter. The question puts the ର୍ in the wrong place, or drops it.

  • ଅନିବାର୍ଯ୍ୟ (cannot be stopped) 2018 Q79 2021 Q1: ର୍ଯ୍ୟ. The wrong options: ଅନିର୍ବାର୍ଯ୍ୟ (extra ର୍), ଅନୀର୍ବାର୍ଯ୍ୟ, ଅନିବାର୍ଯ୍ୟ with a dropped ଯ.
  • ପ୍ରାଦୁର୍ଭାବ (appearance) 2021 Q8: ପ୍ର + ଆଦୁର୍ + ଭାବ. Wrong options: ପାଦୁର୍ଭାବ, ପ୍ରାର୍ଦୁଭାବ, ପାର୍ଦୁଭାବ.
  • ଅନ୍ତର୍ଯାମୀ (knower of hearts) 2021 Q9: ଅନ୍ତର୍ + ଯାମୀ. The ର୍ is on the ତ.
  • ଆଶୀର୍ବାଦ 2021 Q3: ର୍ବ.

Memory help: the ର୍ comes before the letter it belongs with, in Sanskrit. ଅନ୍ତର୍ = "inside" + ଯାମୀ.

1.5Trap 5: the little prefix (ଦୁର, ନିର, ଅନ, ଅବ)

Words made with a prefix (a small word put in front, such as ଅ, ଦୁ, ନି) have a fixed shape. If you do not know which prefix was used, you pick the wrong one.

  • ଦୁରବସ୍ଥା (bad condition) 2021 Q10: ଦୁଃ + ଅବସ୍ଥା = ଦୁରବସ୍ଥା. Not ଦୂରବସ୍ଥା (that puts a "distant" sign), not ଦୁରାବସ୍ଥା.
  • ଅରକ୍ଷିତ (unprotected) 2018 Q76: ଅ + ରକ୍ଷିତ. Short ି, no ୀ.
  • ଅନସୂୟା (a woman with no jealousy) 2018 Q77: ଅନ୍ + ଅସୂୟା, so ଅନସୂୟା. The bait was ଅନୁସୂୟା with the extra ୁ.
  • ନିଷ୍ଫଳ (useless) 2021 Q2 CHECK : the book says the scan is unclear; ନିଷ୍ଫଳ with ଫଳ is the standard spelling.
  • ଓତଃପ୍ରୋତ and ଓତପ୍ରୋତ: 2021 Q4 CHECK : the book keys D ଓତପ୍ରୋତ, but many dictionaries print ଓତଃପ୍ରୋତ; the book's key is ଓତପ୍ରୋତ.

1.6Trap 6: the joined letter has the wrong form

A conjunct is two letters joined, like ତ୍ୱ, ଶ୍ର, ଜ୍ଞ. The question changes one letter in it.

  • ତ୍ୱିଷାଂପତି (Sun, the lord of light) 2025 Q10: ତ୍ୱି + ଷାଂ + ପତି. The wrong options changed ି to ୀ.
  • ଅକ୍ଷୁଣ୍ଣ 2018 Q75 CHECK : the scan is unclear.
  • ନିର୍ଜନ, ନିର୍ଧନ 2018 Q74 CHECK : the scan does not show if the doubled ଜ is printed; ନିର୍ଧନ is also a real word.

1.7Trap 7: ଳ or ଲ

Odia has two "l" letters: ଲ (dental) and ଳ (the cerebral l, tongue curled back). Many Odia words end in ଳ (ଜଳ, ବଳ, ମଙ୍ଗଳ, କମଳ), but a joined ଲ୍ଲ, ଲ୍ୟ uses ଲ (କଲ୍ୟାଣ, ବଲ୍ଲଭ). There is no clean rule; learn the word.

  • ମଧୁଳିକା (a kind of honey flower) 2025 Q9 CHECK : OPSC accepted two answers, B ମଧୁଳିକା and D ମଧୁଲିକା; the book stores B.

1.8Trap 8: anusvara ଂ or the joined ଙ୍ଗ, ଙ୍କ

Some words can be written with the dot ଂ (anusvara) or with a joined ଙ୍ଗ / ଙ୍କ. The key chose the joined form when it was a real choice:

  • ଆନୁଷଙ୍ଗିକ (connected, related) 2025 Q8: ଙ୍ଗ. The wrong options had ଂଗ (ଆନୁସଂଗିକ) or a long ୀ.
  • The dot is right when the sound is a nasal that comes before ପ, ମ, ଶ, ଷ, ସ, ହ and in words like ସଂସ୍କୃତି, ସଂହାର, ସିଂହ, ବିଂଶ, ତ୍ୱିଷାଂପତି.

CHECK : for ସଙ୍ଗୀତ / ସଂଗୀତ and ଅହଙ୍କାର / ଅହଂକାର both forms are printed in books; the papers have not asked these. If you see both in one question, choose the form with ଙ୍ଗ / ଙ୍କ.

1.9Trap 9: ୟ, ଯ and ଯ୍ୟ

The letter ୟ (the "y" that follows a vowel) and the joined ଯ୍ୟ look alike.

  • ପିୟୂଷ: ୟ in the middle.
  • ପର୍ଯ୍ୟବସିତ: ର୍ଯ୍ୟ.
  • ଅନିବାର୍ଯ୍ୟ: ର୍ଯ୍ୟ.
  • ଅନସୂୟା: ୟ.

Rule that helps: a joined ଯ୍ୟ comes at the end of a word (କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ, ଆଚାର୍ଯ୍ୟ, ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟ, ଧୈର୍ଯ୍ୟ); ୟ by itself comes after a vowel sign (ପିୟୂଷ, ଅସୂୟା, ଉତ୍ତରାୟଣ).

1.10Trap 10: a clean word broken into parts

A few wrong options are made by joining the parts of a word in the wrong order, like ଆର୍ଶିବାଦ for ଆଶୀର୍ବାଦ, or ପୂଜ୍ୟଷ୍ପଦ for ପୂଜ୍ୟାସ୍ପଦ 2021 Q6. For these, split the word into its parts and rebuild it:

  • ପୂଜ୍ୟ (worthy) + ଆସ୍ପଦ (place) = ପୂଜ୍ୟାସ୍ପଦ.
  • ଆଶିଷ୍ + ବାଦ → ଆଶୀର୍ବାଦ.
  • ପ୍ର + ବ୍ରଜନ (going) = ପ୍ରବ୍ରଜନ 2021 Q93.
  • ପର୍ଯ୍ୟ + ବସିତ = ପର୍ଯ୍ୟବସିତ 2018 Q80.

Check yourself

Which is right: ପରିଷଦ, ପରୀଷଦ, ପରିସଦ?

ପରିଷଦ (short ି, then ଷ).

Which is right: ଭଗିନୀ or ଭଗୀନୀ?

ଭଗିନୀ. Short ି first, long ୀ at the end.

Which is right: ଦୂରବସ୍ଥା or ଦୁରବସ୍ଥା?

ଦୁରବସ୍ଥା (ଦୁଃ + ଅବସ୍ଥା), not the word for "distance".

A ନ after ର୍, ଷ or ଋ becomes what?

ଣ (ପ୍ରାଣ, ଭୂଷଣ, କାରଣ). It stays ନ if a ଚ, ଜ, ଟ, ଡ, ତ, ଦ type letter breaks the gap, or in a joined word like ଦୁର୍ନୀତି.

Which is right: ଆନୁଷଙ୍ଗିକ or ଆନୁସଂଗିକ?

ଆନୁଷଙ୍ଗିକ.

2.2What the table tells you

Count the traps: short and long i and u are the biggest (about a third of the 33). Then ଷ, ଣ and the ର୍ placement. 3. Synonyms: how the examiner picks

A ପ୍ରତିଶବ୍ଦ (synonym) means "a word with the same meaning". The question gives one word in quotes, for example ‘ମେଘ’ର ପ୍ରତିଶବ୍ଦ (a synonym of "cloud") and four options. You pick the one that means the same.

Odia has a rich stock of synonyms because it borrows from Sanskrit. A cloud is ମେଘ, ଜଳଦ, ଜୀମୂତ, ବଳାହକ, ନୀରଦ, ପୟୋଦ, ଅମ୍ବୁଦ, ଘନ. These are all cloud words. The examiner takes one and puts a different word from the same family among the options.

3.5The three habits of the examiner

1. He asks the same word in different years (ମେଘ twice, ସାପ twice, ଦନୁଜ three times). 2. The right answer is often a lesser-known word: ସରଣୀ for a road, ବହିତ୍ର for a boat, ଦ୍ୱିପ for an elephant. The common word (ବାଟ, ନୌକା, ଗଜ) is the question, not the answer. 3. The bait belongs to the same group, but a different thing: cloud words beside sea words, fire words beside sun words.

6.4Traps to avoid

  • Do not pick a synonym. In ହର୍ଷ, the options ଦୁଃଖ and ଶୋକ and ବିଷାଦ all mean "sorrow". The paper's key is ବିଷାଦ, because the book says so. Learn the partner as a pair.

7.1The ten spelling traps in one line each

1. Short or long ି ୀ ୁ ୂ: female persons and rivers end in ୀ; the first part of ପରୀକ୍ଷା, ପ୍ରତୀକ୍ଷା, ଅଧୀନ has a long ୀ. 2. ଶ, ଷ, ସ: follow the Sanskrit (ପରିଷଦ, ବିଷଣ୍ଣ, ଶ୍ରେଷ୍ଠ). 3. ଣ or ନ: ନ turns to ଣ after ର, ଷ, ଋ (ପ୍ରାଣ, ଭୂଷଣ). 4. ର୍ placement: ଅନିବାର୍ଯ୍ୟ, ପ୍ରାଦୁର୍ଭାବ, ଅନ୍ତର୍ଯାମୀ. 5. Prefix shape: ଦୁରବସ୍ଥା, ଅରକ୍ଷିତ, ଅନସୂୟା. 6. Conjunct form: ତ୍ୱ, ଜ୍ୱ, ସ୍ୱ. 7. ଳ or ଲ: learn the word (ମଙ୍ଗଳ, ଜଳ, କଲ୍ୟାଣ). 8. ଙ୍ଗ or ଂ: ଆନୁଷଙ୍ଗିକ. 9. ର୍ଯ୍ୟ at the end: କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ, ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟ, ଆଚାର୍ଯ୍ୟ. 10. Split the word: prefix + root.
