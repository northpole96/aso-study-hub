This page is the front door to everything made for the ASO exam. The written exam is planned for 17 January 2027 (the date is still tentative). Every page below opens on its own, works on a phone, and keeps your answers on this device.

Start with the syllabus: it shows each topic the commission lists and which notes or tests cover it, so the gaps are easy to see. Then read the past papers, practise with the mocks and revise from the guides. Scores from finished mocks show up here on their own.

Part 03 · PracticeScores kept on this device

Mocks and practice tests

Full 100-question mocks follow the real paper: 90 minutes for GA, +1 for a right answer and −0.25 for a wrong one. The English mocks form a chain. After each one, press Copy answers on the result screen and paste the report to Claude. The next mock then brings back every question you missed, plus new questions that test the same rules.

Part 04 · NotesRead and revise

Guides and notes

The GA Reference Manual is the main book. It follows the ten Paper I topics, explains each in simple English, and ends every chapter with the questions most likely to come next. The Current Affairs Manual covers 2026 news in the way the paper asks about it.
